# 399-left-hanging Left Hanging

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I got left hanging on ...
- 意思: 被晾在那了
- 用法: 用于说等待答复或帮助但没有下文。

## 例句

1. I got left hanging on the support ticket for weeks.
   - 我的支持工单被晾了好几周。

2. I got left hanging on the job interview follow-up.
   - 面试后续被晾在那了。

3. I got left hanging on the repair request.
   - 维修申请被晾在那了。

4. I got left hanging on the refund process.
   - 退款流程被晾在那了。

5. I got left hanging on the contract approval.
   - 合同审批被晾在那了。
