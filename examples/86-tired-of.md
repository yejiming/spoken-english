# 86-tired-of Tired Of

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm honestly tired of ...
- 意思: 开始厌倦。
- 用法: 用于说你腻了。

## 例句

1. I'm honestly tired of speaking up in meetings
   - 说实话，我已经受够了在会议上发言。

2. I'm honestly tired of spending money on that
   - 说实话，我已经受够了为那个花钱。

3. I'm honestly tired of this kind of music
   - 说实话，我已经受够了这种音乐。

4. I'm honestly tired of trying something new
   - 说实话，我已经受够了尝试新事物。

5. I'm honestly tired of the way this feels
   - 说实话，我已经受够了这种感觉。
