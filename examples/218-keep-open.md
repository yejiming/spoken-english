# 218-keep-open Keep Open

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm just trying to keep ... open.
- 意思: 想把时间空出
- 用法: 用于表达你想保留某段时间不做安排。

## 例句

1. I'm just trying to keep my Saturday evening open.
   - 我只是想把周六晚上空出来。

2. I'm just trying to keep my options open.
   - 我只是想多留几个选择。

3. I'm just trying to keep next Friday open.
   - 我只是想把下周五空出来。

4. I'm just trying to keep the afternoon open.
   - 我只是想把下午空出来。

5. I'm just trying to keep a couple of days open.
   - 我只是想空出来几天。
