# 456-proud-of Proud Of

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm proud of myself for ...
- 意思: 为自己骄傲
- 用法: 用于说对自己完成某事感到自豪。

## 例句

1. I'm proud of myself for finishing the marathon.
   - 跑完马拉松我为自己骄傲。

2. I'm proud of myself for sticking to the budget.
   - 坚持预算我为自己骄傲。

3. I'm proud of myself for speaking up in the meeting.
   - 在会上发言我为自己骄傲。

4. I'm proud of myself for quitting that bad habit.
   - 戒掉坏习惯我为自己骄傲。

5. I'm proud of myself for learning to code this year.
   - 今年学会编程我为自己骄傲。
