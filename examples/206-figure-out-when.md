# 206-figure-out-when Figure Out When

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm trying to figure out when to ...
- 意思: 在想什么时候
- 用法: 用于表达你在考虑合适的时间做某件事。

## 例句

1. I'm trying to figure out when to schedule the dinner.
   - 我在想什么时候安排晚饭合适。

2. I'm trying to figure out when to visit my parents.
   - 我在想什么时候去看望父母。

3. I'm trying to figure out when to take a day off.
   - 我在想什么时候请一天假。

4. I'm trying to figure out when to meet up with everyone.
   - 我在想什么时候跟大家碰面。

5. I'm trying to figure out when to throw the surprise party.
   - 我在想什么时候办惊喜派对。
