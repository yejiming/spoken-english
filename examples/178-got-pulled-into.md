# 178-got-pulled-into Got Pulled Into

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I got pulled into ... unexpectedly.
- 意思: 被临时拉去了
- 用法: 用于说你意外地被叫去参加某个事情。

## 例句

1. I got pulled into a last-minute meeting unexpectedly.
   - 我被临时拉去开了个紧急会议。

2. I got pulled into a client call unexpectedly.
   - 我被临时拉去接了个客户电话。

3. I got pulled into fixing a production issue unexpectedly.
   - 我被临时拉去修了个生产环境的问题。

4. I got pulled into covering someone's shift unexpectedly.
   - 我被临时拉去替人顶班了。

5. I got pulled into a brainstorming session unexpectedly.
   - 我被临时拉去参加头脑风暴了。
