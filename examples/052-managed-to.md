# 052-managed-to Managed To

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I managed to ... before leaving.
- 意思: 走前总算做了
- 用法: 用于表示出门之前总算来得及完成了某事。

## 例句

1. I managed to do the dishes before leaving.
   - 我走之前总算把碗洗了。

2. I managed to take out the trash before leaving.
   - 我走之前总算把垃圾倒了。

3. I managed to pack lunch before leaving.
   - 我走之前总算把午饭带上了。

4. I managed to feed the cat before leaving.
   - 我走之前总算把猫喂了。

5. I managed to tidy up before leaving.
   - 我走之前总算收拾了一下。
