# 89-worth-it Worth It

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I think it's worth ...
- 意思: 值得一试。
- 用法: 用于评价值不值得。

## 例句

1. I think it's worth taking a break
   - 我觉得休息一下值得。

2. I think it's worth trying the spicy noodles
   - 我觉得尝试辣面值得。

3. I think it's worth having more time
   - 我觉得拥有更多时间值得。

4. I think it's worth moving to a new place
   - 我觉得搬去新地方值得。

5. I think it's worth keeping things simple
   - 我觉得保持简单值得。
