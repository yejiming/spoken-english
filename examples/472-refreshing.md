# 472-refreshing Refreshing

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It's refreshing to see ...
- 意思: 让人耳目一新
- 用法: 用于说某事令人感到新鲜舒适。

## 例句

1. It's refreshing to see a movie with an original plot.
   - 看到有原创剧情的电影让人耳目一新。

2. It's refreshing to see someone be honest about mistakes.
   - 看到有人坦诚面对错误让人耳目一新。

3. It's refreshing to see a company that values employees.
   - 看到重视员工的公司让人耳目一新。

4. It's refreshing to see young people volunteering.
   - 看到年轻人做志愿者让人耳目一新。

5. It's refreshing to see a restaurant with a simple menu.
   - 看到菜单简洁的餐厅让人耳目一新。
