# 88-don-t-mind Don’t Mind

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I don't mind ...
- 意思: 态度中性。
- 用法: 用于说你不反感。

## 例句

1. I don't mind the new restaurant
   - 我不介意那家新餐厅。

2. I don't mind working from home
   - 我不介意在家办公。

3. I don't mind people being late
   - 我不介意别人迟到。

4. I don't mind taking a break
   - 我不介意休息一下。

5. I don't mind trying the spicy noodles
   - 我不介意尝试辣面。
