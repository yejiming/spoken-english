# 081-letting-it-slide Letting It Slide

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm letting ... slide for now.
- 意思: 先放一放
- 用法: 用于表示暂时不去管某件事，先搁置一下。

## 例句

1. I'm letting the messy kitchen slide for now.
   - 厨房乱就先不管了。

2. I'm letting my workout schedule slide for now.
   - 健身计划先放一放吧。

3. I'm letting the pile of laundry slide for now.
   - 那堆衣服先不洗了。

4. I'm letting my diet slide for now.
   - 减肥的事先放一放吧。

5. I'm letting the dishes slide for now.
   - 碗先不洗了。
