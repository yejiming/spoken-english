# 187-clocked-out-after Clocked Out After

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I clocked out right after ...
- 意思: 一结束就下班了
- 用法: 用于说你在某件事结束后就马上下班了。

## 例句

1. I clocked out right after the meeting.
   - 会议一结束我就下班了。

2. I clocked out right after submitting the report.
   - 交完报告我就下班了。

3. I clocked out right after my last class.
   - 最后一节课结束我就走了。

4. I clocked out right after the presentation.
   - 汇报一结束我就下班了。

5. I clocked out right after finishing the audit.
   - 审计做完我就下班了。
