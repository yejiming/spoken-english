# 429-overrated Overrated

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I think ... is overrated.
- 意思: 觉得被高估了
- 用法: 用于说觉得某事物名过其实。

## 例句

1. I think brunch is overrated.
   - 我觉得早午餐被高估了。

2. I think expensive headphones are overrated.
   - 我觉得贵的耳机被高估了。

3. I think hustle culture is overrated.
   - 我觉得加班文化被高估了。

4. I think designer clothes are overrated.
   - 我觉得名牌衣服被高估了。

5. I think going viral is overrated.
   - 我觉得走红被高估了。
