# 329-gave-me-wrong Gave Me Wrong

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: They gave me the wrong ...
- 意思: 给错了
- 用法: 用于说对方给了你错误的东西。

## 例句

1. They gave me the wrong order at the drive-through.
   - 免下车窗口给错了我的订单。

2. They gave me the wrong size shoes.
   - 他们给了我错码的鞋。

3. They gave me the wrong prescription.
   - 他们给了我错误的处方。

4. They gave me the wrong room key at the hotel.
   - 酒店给了我错误的房间钥匙。

5. They gave me the wrong tracking number.
   - 他们给了我错误的快递单号。
