# 383-wrong-settings Wrong Settings

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I had the wrong settings for ...
- 意思: 设置搞错了
- 用法: 用于说用了错误的配置。

## 例句

1. I had the wrong settings for the camera exposure.
   - 我相机的曝光设置搞错了。

2. I had the wrong settings for the email notifications.
   - 我邮件通知的设置搞错了。

3. I had the wrong settings for the oven temperature.
   - 我烤箱的温度设置搞错了。

4. I had the wrong settings for the display resolution.
   - 我显示器的分辨率设置搞错了。

5. I had the wrong settings for the privacy options.
   - 我隐私选项的设置搞错了。
