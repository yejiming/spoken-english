# 436-means-a-lot Means A Lot

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It means a lot that you ...
- 意思: 真的很感动
- 用法: 用于说某人的行为让你很感动。

## 例句

1. It means a lot that you stayed late to help me.
   - 你留下来帮我加班真的很感动。

2. It means a lot that you remembered my birthday.
   - 你记得我的生日真的很感动。

3. It means a lot that you drove all the way here.
   - 你大老远开车过来真的很感动。

4. It means a lot that you believed in me from the start.
   - 你从一开始就相信我真的很感动。

5. It means a lot that you checked in on me.
   - 你来关心我真的很感动。
