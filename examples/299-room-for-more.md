# 299-room-for-more Room For More

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Do we have room for ... more people?
- 意思: 还能加人吗
- 用法: 用于确认活动是否还有多余名额。

## 例句

1. Do we have room for two more people?
   - 还能再加两个人吗？

2. Do we have room for one more people?
   - 还能再加一个人吗？

3. Do we have room for a few more people?
   - 还能再加几个人吗？

4. Do we have room for three more people?
   - 还能再加三个人吗？

5. Do we have room for a couple more people?
   - 还能再加几个人吗？
