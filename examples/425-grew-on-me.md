# 425-grew-on-me Grew On Me

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It kinda grew on me — ...
- 意思: 慢慢喜欢上了
- 用法: 用于说一开始不喜欢后来慢慢接受。

## 例句

1. It kinda grew on me — this new coffee flavor is nice.
   - 慢慢喜欢上了，这个新口味咖啡不错。

2. It kinda grew on me — the show gets better after episode three.
   - 慢慢喜欢上了，这剧第三集之后越来越好。

3. It kinda grew on me — working out in the morning is great.
   - 慢慢喜欢上了，早上锻炼真不错。

4. It kinda grew on me — I actually like this haircut now.
   - 慢慢喜欢上了，这个发型现在觉得挺好。

5. It kinda grew on me — living in a small town is peaceful.
   - 慢慢喜欢上了，住小城镇挺安静的。
