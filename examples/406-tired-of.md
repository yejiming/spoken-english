# 406-tired-of Tired Of

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm honestly tired of ...
- 意思: 已经厌倦
- 用法: 用于坦诚表达对某事的疲惫和厌烦。

## 例句

1. I'm honestly tired of eating the same thing every day.
   - 说实话，我已经受够了每天吃一样的东西。

2. I'm honestly tired of dealing with all this paperwork.
   - 说实话，我已经受够了处理这些文件。

3. I'm honestly tired of waiting for things to change.
   - 说实话，我已经受够了等待事情改变。

4. I'm honestly tired of explaining myself over and over.
   - 说实话，我已经受够了反复解释自己。

5. I'm honestly tired of the constant noise from next door.
   - 说实话，我已经受够了隔壁持续不断的噪音。
