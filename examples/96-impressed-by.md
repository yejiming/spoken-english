# 96-impressed-by Impressed By

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm impressed by ...
- 意思: 觉得很厉害。
- 用法: 用于表达赞叹。

## 例句

1. I'm impressed by trying the spicy noodles
   - 我对尝试辣面印象很深。

2. I'm impressed by having more time
   - 我对拥有更多时间印象很深。

3. I'm impressed by moving to a new place
   - 我对搬去新地方印象很深。

4. I'm impressed by keeping things simple
   - 我对保持简单印象很深。

5. I'm impressed by a crowded room
   - 我对一个拥挤的房间印象很深。
