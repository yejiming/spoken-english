# 256-no-show No Show

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: They were a no-show for ...
- 意思: 人没出现
- 用法: 用于描述某人没有出席约好的活动。

## 例句

1. They were a no-show for the dinner.
   - 他们晚饭没来。

2. They were a no-show for the meeting.
   - 他们约好的见面没出现。

3. They were a no-show for the party.
   - 他们派对没到。

4. They were a no-show for the movie.
   - 他们看电影没来。

5. They were a no-show for the group photo.
   - 拍合照的时候他们没出现。
