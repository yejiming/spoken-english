# 033-finally-got-to Finally Got To

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I finally got around to ...
- 意思: 终于腾出手做
- 用法: 用于表示拖了很久终于开始做某件事。

## 例句

1. I finally got around to organizing my closet.
   - 我终于腾出手来整理衣柜了。

2. I finally got around to fixing the leaky faucet.
   - 我终于腾出手来修漏水的水龙头了。

3. I finally got around to reading that book.
   - 我终于腾出手来看那本书了。

4. I finally got around to cleaning the garage.
   - 我终于腾出手来清理车库了。

5. I finally got around to updating my phone.
   - 我终于腾出手来更新手机了。
