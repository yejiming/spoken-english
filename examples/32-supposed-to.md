# 32-supposed-to Supposed To

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I'm supposed to ... by noon.
- 意思: 按时完成。
- 用法: 用于说你按计划应该做什么。

## 例句

1. I'm supposed to class prep by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前备课。

2. I'm supposed to the comments by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前这些批注。

3. I'm supposed to the handout by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前讲义。

4. I'm supposed to the summary by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前总结。

5. I'm supposed to my notes by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前我的笔记。
