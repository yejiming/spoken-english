# 032-keep-forgetting Keep Forgetting

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I keep forgetting to ...
- 意思: 老是忘记做
- 用法: 用于表示反复忘记去做某件事。

## 例句

1. I keep forgetting to buy milk.
   - 我老是忘记买牛奶。

2. I keep forgetting to take my vitamins.
   - 我老是忘记吃维生素。

3. I keep forgetting to lock the door.
   - 我老是忘记锁门。

4. I keep forgetting to charge my headphones.
   - 我老是忘记给耳机充电。

5. I keep forgetting to bring my umbrella.
   - 我老是忘记带伞。
