# 130-got-assigned Got Assigned

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I got assigned to ...
- 意思: 被分配到
- 用法: 用于说你被安排负责某项任务或团队。

## 例句

1. I got assigned to the new marketing campaign.
   - 我被分配到新的营销活动了。

2. I got assigned to a different team this quarter.
   - 这个季度我被分到了另一个组。

3. I got assigned to cover the front desk today.
   - 今天我被安排去看前台。

4. I got assigned to the night shift again.
   - 我又被排到夜班了。

5. I got assigned to lead the onboarding for new hires.
   - 我被安排带新员工入职培训。
