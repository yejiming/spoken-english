# 140-can-t-figure-out Can't Figure Out

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I can't figure out how to ...
- 意思: 搞不明白怎么
- 用法: 用于说你不知道如何做某件事。

## 例句

1. I can't figure out how to use this new software.
   - 我搞不明白怎么用这个新软件。

2. I can't figure out how to fix the formula.
   - 我搞不明白怎么修这个公式。

3. I can't figure out how to merge these documents.
   - 我搞不明白怎么合并这些文档。

4. I can't figure out how to solve this problem.
   - 我搞不明白怎么解这道题。

5. I can't figure out how to set up the VPN.
   - 我搞不明白怎么设置VPN。
