# 42-want-to-tonight Want To Tonight

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Do you want to ... tonight?
- 意思: 轻松邀约。
- 用法: 用于直接发出邀请。

## 例句

1. Do you want to go for a walk tonight?
   - 你今晚想散步吗？

2. Do you want to check out the new place tonight?
   - 你今晚想去看看那家新店吗？

3. Do you want to hang out tonight?
   - 你今晚想一起待着吗？

4. Do you want to celebrate a little tonight?
   - 你今晚想小小庆祝一下吗？

5. Do you want to have a quick chat tonight?
   - 你今晚想聊几句吗？
