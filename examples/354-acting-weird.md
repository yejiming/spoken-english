# 354-acting-weird Acting Weird

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: My ... is acting weird.
- 意思: 表现异常
- 用法: 用于说设备行为不正常。

## 例句

1. My phone is acting weird — apps keep closing.
   - 我的手机不正常，应用老是自动关。

2. My car is acting weird when I brake.
   - 我的车刹车时不太正常。

3. My smart speaker is acting weird today.
   - 我的智能音箱今天不太正常。

4. My laptop is acting weird after the update.
   - 更新后我的笔记本不太正常。

5. My printer is acting weird — it prints blank pages.
   - 我的打印机不正常，打出来是白纸。
