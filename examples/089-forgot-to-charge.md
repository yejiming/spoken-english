# 089-forgot-to-charge Forgot To Charge

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I forgot to charge my ...
- 意思: 忘了充电
- 用法: 用于表示忘记给某个电子设备充电了。

## 例句

1. I forgot to charge my phone last night.
   - 我昨晚忘了给手机充电。

2. I forgot to charge my earbuds.
   - 我忘了给耳机充电。

3. I forgot to charge my laptop.
   - 我忘了给笔记本充电。

4. I forgot to charge my smartwatch.
   - 我忘了给智能手表充电。

5. I forgot to charge my electric toothbrush.
   - 我忘了给电动牙刷充电。
