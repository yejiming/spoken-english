# 104-help-me-with Help Me With

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: Could you help me with ...?
- 意思: 请帮我一下
- 用法: 用于礼貌地向别人请求帮助。

## 例句

1. Could you help me with this math problem?
   - 你能帮我看看这道数学题吗？

2. Could you help me with the slide design?
   - 你能帮我弄一下幻灯片设计吗？

3. Could you help me with proofreading this essay?
   - 你能帮我校对一下这篇文章吗？

4. Could you help me with the database query?
   - 你能帮我看看这个数据库查询吗？

5. Could you help me with setting up the projector?
   - 你能帮我装一下投影仪吗？
