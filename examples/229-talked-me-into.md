# 229-talked-me-into Talked Me Into

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: They talked me into ...
- 意思: 被说服了
- 用法: 用于表示别人说服了你去做某事。

## 例句

1. They talked me into going out tonight.
   - 他们说服我今晚出去玩。

2. They talked me into trying bungee jumping.
   - 他们说服我去试蹦极。

3. They talked me into joining the road trip.
   - 他们说服我加入了自驾游。

4. They talked me into staying for one more drink.
   - 他们说服我再喝一杯。

5. They talked me into signing up for the run.
   - 他们说服我报名跑步活动。
