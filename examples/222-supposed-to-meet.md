# 222-supposed-to-meet Supposed To Meet

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm supposed to meet ... later.
- 意思: 待会要见人
- 用法: 用于告知别人你稍后有约。

## 例句

1. I'm supposed to meet Jake later.
   - 我待会要见杰克。

2. I'm supposed to meet my sister later.
   - 我待会要见我姐。

3. I'm supposed to meet the group later.
   - 我待会要跟大家碰面。

4. I'm supposed to meet my coworker later.
   - 我待会要见我同事。

5. I'm supposed to meet an old friend later.
   - 我待会要见一个老朋友。
