# 494-makes-sense Makes Sense

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It makes sense that ...
- 意思: 合情合理
- 用法: 用于说某事在逻辑上说得通。

## 例句

1. It makes sense that they moved the meeting to Monday.
   - 他们把会议改到周一很合情合理。

2. It makes sense that the price went up with demand.
   - 需求增加价格上涨很合情合理。

3. It makes sense that she's exhausted after that schedule.
   - 那种日程之后她累了很合情合理。

4. It makes sense that they paused the rollout.
   - 他们暂停推广很合情合理。

5. It makes sense that people prefer convenience.
   - 人们喜欢方便很合情合理。
