# 288-staying-in-instead Staying In Instead

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm staying in instead of going to ...
- 意思: 不出门了
- 用法: 用于表示你选择留在家里而不出门。

## 例句

1. I'm staying in instead of going to the bar.
   - 我不去酒吧了，待在家。

2. I'm staying in instead of going to the party.
   - 我不去派对了，待在家。

3. I'm staying in instead of going to the concert.
   - 我不去演唱会了，待在家。

4. I'm staying in instead of going to dinner.
   - 我不出去吃饭了，待在家。

5. I'm staying in instead of going to the club.
   - 我不去夜店了，待在家。
