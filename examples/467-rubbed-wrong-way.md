# 467-rubbed-wrong-way Rubbed Wrong Way

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It rubbed me the wrong way when ...
- 意思: 让我很不舒服
- 用法: 用于说某事让你感到反感。

## 例句

1. It rubbed me the wrong way when he dismissed my idea.
   - 他否定我的想法让我很不舒服。

2. It rubbed me the wrong way when they showed up late.
   - 他们迟到让我很不舒服。

3. It rubbed me the wrong way when she didn't say thanks.
   - 她没说谢谢让我很不舒服。

4. It rubbed me the wrong way when they ignored the email.
   - 他们无视邮件让我很不舒服。

5. It rubbed me the wrong way when he took credit for it.
   - 他把功劳揽走让我很不舒服。
