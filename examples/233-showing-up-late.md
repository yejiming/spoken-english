# 233-showing-up-late Showing Up Late

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I might show up late to ...
- 意思: 可能会迟到
- 用法: 用于提前告知对方你可能晚到。

## 例句

1. I might show up late to the party.
   - 我可能去派对会迟到。

2. I might show up late to dinner.
   - 我晚饭可能会迟到。

3. I might show up late to the game.
   - 我看比赛可能晚到。

4. I might show up late to brunch.
   - 早午餐我可能会迟到。

5. I might show up late to the meetup.
   - 聚会我可能晚到一会儿。
