# 460-guilty-about Guilty About

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I feel guilty about ...
- 意思: 感到内疚
- 用法: 用于说对某事心怀愧疚。

## 例句

1. I feel guilty about not calling my parents more often.
   - 不常给爸妈打电话让我感到内疚。

2. I feel guilty about binge-watching TV all weekend.
   - 整个周末追剧让我感到内疚。

3. I feel guilty about cancelling on my friend last minute.
   - 临时取消跟朋友的约让我感到内疚。

4. I feel guilty about eating junk food for three days.
   - 连续三天吃垃圾食品让我感到内疚。

5. I feel guilty about not finishing the book I borrowed.
   - 借的书没看完让我感到内疚。
