# 377-on-and-off On And Off

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: It's been on and off with ...
- 意思: 时好时坏
- 用法: 用于说问题断断续续出现。

## 例句

1. It's been on and off with the internet connection.
   - 网络连接时好时坏。

2. It's been on and off with the hot water.
   - 热水时好时坏。

3. It's been on and off with the AC all summer.
   - 整个夏天空调时好时坏。

4. It's been on and off with the car engine light.
   - 车的发动机指示灯时好时坏。

5. It's been on and off with the signal in this area.
   - 这个区域的信号时好时坏。
