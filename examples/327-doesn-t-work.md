# 327-doesn-t-work Doesn't Work

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I tried ... but it doesn't work.
- 意思: 试了没用
- 用法: 用于说尝试后发现没有效果。

## 例句

1. I tried restarting but it doesn't work.
   - 我试了重启但没用。

2. I tried clearing the cache but it doesn't work.
   - 我试了清缓存但没用。

3. I tried a different charger but it doesn't work.
   - 我试了另一个充电器但没用。

4. I tried updating the driver but it doesn't work.
   - 我试了更新驱动但没用。

5. I tried reinstalling the app but it doesn't work.
   - 我试了重装应用但没用。
