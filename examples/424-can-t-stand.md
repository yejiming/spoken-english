# 424-can-t-stand Can't Stand

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can't stand when ...
- 意思: 最受不了
- 用法: 用于说极其讨厌某事。

## 例句

1. I can't stand when people chew with their mouth open.
   - 我最受不了别人吃东西嘴巴不闭。

2. I can't stand when my phone dies at the worst moment.
   - 我最受不了手机在最关键时刻没电。

3. I can't stand when people cut in line.
   - 我最受不了有人插队。

4. I can't stand when meetings drag on for no reason.
   - 我最受不了会议毫无理由地拖。

5. I can't stand when the Wi-Fi drops during a call.
   - 我最受不了打电话时Wi-Fi断了。
