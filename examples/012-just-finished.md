# 012-just-finished Just Finished

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I just finished ...
- 意思: 刚做完的事
- 用法: 用于说你刚结束某件事。

## 例句

1. I just finished cleaning the bathroom.
   - 我刚打扫完卫生间。

2. I just finished reading that article.
   - 我刚读完那篇文章。

3. I just finished cooking dinner.
   - 我刚做好晚饭。

4. I just finished folding the laundry.
   - 我刚叠好衣服。

5. I just finished walking the dog.
   - 我刚遛完狗。
