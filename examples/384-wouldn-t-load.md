# 384-wouldn-t-load Wouldn't Load

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: The ... wouldn't load at all.
- 意思: 完全加载不出
- 用法: 用于说页面或内容完全无法加载。

## 例句

1. The website wouldn't load at all on my phone.
   - 这个网站在我手机上完全加载不出。

2. The video wouldn't load at all no matter what I tried.
   - 不管怎么试视频都加载不出来。

3. The images wouldn't load at all in the email.
   - 邮件里的图片完全加载不出来。

4. The game wouldn't load at all after the patch.
   - 打补丁后游戏完全加载不出来。

5. The dashboard wouldn't load at all this morning.
   - 今天早上仪表盘完全加载不出来。
