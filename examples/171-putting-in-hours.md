# 171-putting-in-hours Putting In Hours

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I've been putting in extra hours on ...
- 意思: 一直在加班做
- 用法: 用于说你在某件事上投入了大量额外时间。

## 例句

1. I've been putting in extra hours on the annual report.
   - 我一直在加班写年度报告。

2. I've been putting in extra hours on studying for the bar exam.
   - 我一直在加班复习律师资格考试。

3. I've been putting in extra hours on the product launch.
   - 我一直在加班准备产品发布。

4. I've been putting in extra hours on the database migration.
   - 我一直在加班做数据库迁移。

5. I've been putting in extra hours on the grant proposal.
   - 我一直在加班写基金申请书。
