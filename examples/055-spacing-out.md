# 055-spacing-out Spacing Out

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I keep spacing out during ...
- 意思: 老是走神
- 用法: 用于描述你在做某事时总是注意力不集中。

## 例句

1. I keep spacing out during meetings.
   - 我开会时老是走神。

2. I keep spacing out during online classes.
   - 我上网课时老是走神。

3. I keep spacing out during conversations.
   - 我聊天时老是走神。

4. I keep spacing out during my commute.
   - 我通勤时老是走神。

5. I keep spacing out during breakfast.
   - 我吃早饭时老是走神。
