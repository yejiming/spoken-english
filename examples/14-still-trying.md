# 14-still-trying Still Trying

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm still trying to ...
- 意思: 还在适应或练习。
- 用法: 用于说你还没完全搞定。

## 例句

1. I'm still trying to wind down
   - 我还在努力放松一下。

2. I'm still trying to make coffee
   - 我还在努力泡咖啡。

3. I'm still trying to check my phone
   - 我还在努力看手机。

4. I'm still trying to take a shower
   - 我还在努力洗个澡。

5. I'm still trying to get dressed
   - 我还在努力穿好衣服。
