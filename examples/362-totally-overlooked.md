# 362-totally-overlooked Totally Overlooked

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I totally overlooked ...
- 意思: 完全忽略了
- 用法: 用于说完全遗漏了某件事。

## 例句

1. I totally overlooked the deadline for the submission.
   - 我完全忽略了提交截止日期。

2. I totally overlooked that setting in the menu.
   - 我完全忽略了菜单里那个设置。

3. I totally overlooked the fine print in the contract.
   - 我完全忽略了合同里的小字条款。

4. I totally overlooked the error message at the bottom.
   - 我完全忽略了底部的错误提示。

5. I totally overlooked the meeting on my calendar.
   - 我完全忽略了日历上的那个会议。
