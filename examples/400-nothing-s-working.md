# 400-nothing-s-working Nothing's Working

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: Nothing's working with ...
- 意思: 什么都不好使
- 用法: 用于说所有方法都行不通。

## 例句

1. Nothing's working with this old laptop anymore.
   - 这台旧笔记本什么都不好使了。

2. Nothing's working with the new update they pushed.
   - 他们推的新更新什么都不好使。

3. Nothing's working with my internet today.
   - 我今天的网什么都不好使。

4. Nothing's working with the printer setup.
   - 打印机设置什么都不好使。

5. Nothing's working with the smart home devices.
   - 智能家居设备什么都不好使。
