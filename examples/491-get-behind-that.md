# 491-get-behind-that Get Behind That

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can totally get behind ...
- 意思: 完全支持
- 用法: 用于说完全支持或赞同某想法。

## 例句

1. I can totally get behind a four-day workweek.
   - 四天工作制我完全支持。

2. I can totally get behind more outdoor meetings.
   - 多在户外开会我完全支持。

3. I can totally get behind investing in education.
   - 投资教育我完全支持。

4. I can totally get behind bringing lunch from home.
   - 自己带午饭我完全支持。

5. I can totally get behind flexible working hours.
   - 弹性工作时间我完全支持。
