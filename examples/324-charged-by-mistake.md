# 324-charged-by-mistake Charged By Mistake

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I got charged for ... by mistake.
- 意思: 被误扣费了
- 用法: 用于说被错误收费。

## 例句

1. I got charged for a subscription I cancelled by mistake.
   - 我被误扣了已取消的订阅费。

2. I got charged for two orders by mistake.
   - 我被误扣了两份订单的钱。

3. I got charged for premium shipping by mistake.
   - 我被误扣了加急运费。

4. I got charged for someone else's meal by mistake.
   - 我被误扣了别人的餐费。

5. I got charged for an extra night at the hotel by mistake.
   - 我被误扣了酒店多一晚的费用。
