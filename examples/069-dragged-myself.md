# 069-dragged-myself Dragged Myself

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I dragged myself out of bed to ...
- 意思: 硬撑着起床做
- 用法: 用于形容非常不情愿地起床去做某件事。

## 例句

1. I dragged myself out of bed to go to the gym.
   - 我硬撑着起来去了健身房。

2. I dragged myself out of bed to walk the dog.
   - 我硬撑着起来去遛了狗。

3. I dragged myself out of bed to make breakfast.
   - 我硬撑着起来做了早饭。

4. I dragged myself out of bed to catch the bus.
   - 我硬撑着起来赶公交。

5. I dragged myself out of bed to take a shower.
   - 我硬撑着起来去洗了个澡。
