# 210-hoping-to Hoping To

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm hoping to ... this weekend.
- 意思: 周末希望能做
- 用法: 用于表达你对这个周末的期望和计划。

## 例句

1. I'm hoping to catch up with some old friends this weekend.
   - 我这周末想跟几个老朋友聚聚。

2. I'm hoping to finally check out that hiking trail this weekend.
   - 我这周末终于想去走走那条步道。

3. I'm hoping to sleep in and do nothing this weekend.
   - 我这周末想好好睡个懒觉啥也不干。

4. I'm hoping to hit the beach this weekend.
   - 我这周末想去海边玩。

5. I'm hoping to go to the farmers' market this weekend.
   - 我这周末想去逛农贸市场。
