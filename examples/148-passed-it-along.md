# 148-passed-it-along Passed It Along

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I passed ... along to the team.
- 意思: 转给团队了
- 用法: 用于说你已经把信息或任务传递给了团队。

## 例句

1. I passed the client's request along to the team.
   - 我把客户的需求转给团队了。

2. I passed the updated schedule along to the team.
   - 我把更新后的日程转给团队了。

3. I passed the meeting notes along to the team.
   - 我把会议记录转给团队了。

4. I passed the new guidelines along to the team.
   - 我把新的指导方针转给团队了。

5. I passed the bug report along to the team.
   - 我把bug报告转给团队了。
