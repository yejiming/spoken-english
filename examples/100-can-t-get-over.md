# 100-can-t-get-over Can’t Get Over

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I just can't get over ...
- 意思: 很难释怀。
- 用法: 用于说你一直惦记。

## 例句

1. I just can't get over this kind of music
   - 我就是忘不掉这种音乐。

2. I just can't get over trying something new
   - 我就是忘不掉尝试新事物。

3. I just can't get over the way this feels
   - 我就是忘不掉这种感觉。

4. I just can't get over staying home tonight
   - 我就是忘不掉今晚待在家。

5. I just can't get over the new restaurant
   - 我就是忘不掉那家新餐厅。
