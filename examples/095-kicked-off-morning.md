# 095-kicked-off-morning Kicked Off Morning

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I kicked off my morning with ...
- 意思: 用某事开启早晨
- 用法: 用于描述以某个活动作为一天的开始。

## 例句

1. I kicked off my morning with a cold shower.
   - 我用一个冷水澡开启了早晨。

2. I kicked off my morning with a smoothie.
   - 我用一杯奶昔开启了早晨。

3. I kicked off my morning with some stretching.
   - 我用拉伸运动开启了早晨。

4. I kicked off my morning with a walk around the block.
   - 我用绕街区散步开启了早晨。

5. I kicked off my morning with a big breakfast.
   - 我用一顿丰盛早餐开启了早晨。
