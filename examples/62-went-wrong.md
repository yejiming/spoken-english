# 62-went-wrong Went Wrong

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: Something went wrong with ...
- 意思: 事情出错了。
- 用法: 用于说哪里出故障。

## 例句

1. Something went wrong with connect the printer
   - 连接打印机出了问题。

2. Something went wrong with save the changes
   - 保存更改出了问题。

3. Something went wrong with clear the cache
   - 清理缓存出了问题。

4. Something went wrong with get the video to play
   - 让视频播放出了问题。

5. Something went wrong with reset the password
   - 重设密码出了问题。
