# 469-not-bothered Not Bothered

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm not bothered by ...
- 意思: 不在意
- 用法: 用于说某事不会困扰你。

## 例句

1. I'm not bothered by the rain — I actually like it.
   - 下雨我不在意，其实我挺喜欢的。

2. I'm not bothered by eating alone at restaurants.
   - 一个人在餐厅吃饭我不在意。

3. I'm not bothered by what people think of my style.
   - 别人怎么看我的风格我不在意。

4. I'm not bothered by the noise when I have headphones on.
   - 戴着耳机噪音我不在意。

5. I'm not bothered by staying in on a Friday night.
   - 周五晚上待在家我不在意。
