# 389-can-t-keep-up Can't Keep Up

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I can't keep up with everything going wrong with ...
- 意思: 忙不过来了
- 用法: 用于说问题太多应付不过来。

## 例句

1. I can't keep up with everything going wrong with the move.
   - 搬家出的问题太多我忙不过来了。

2. I can't keep up with everything going wrong with the event.
   - 活动出的问题太多我忙不过来了。

3. I can't keep up with everything going wrong with the launch.
   - 上线出的问题太多我忙不过来了。

4. I can't keep up with everything going wrong with the house.
   - 房子出的问题太多我忙不过来了。

5. I can't keep up with everything going wrong with the order.
   - 订单出的问题太多我忙不过来了。
