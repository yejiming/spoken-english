# 333-stuck-in-a-loop Stuck In A Loop

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm stuck in a loop trying to ...
- 意思: 卡在死循环里
- 用法: 用于说反复尝试却没进展。

## 例句

1. I'm stuck in a loop trying to reset my password.
   - 我重置密码卡在死循环里了。

2. I'm stuck in a loop trying to verify my identity.
   - 我验证身份卡在死循环里了。

3. I'm stuck in a loop trying to cancel the subscription.
   - 我取消订阅卡在死循环里了。

4. I'm stuck in a loop trying to reach customer support.
   - 我联系客服卡在死循环里了。

5. I'm stuck in a loop trying to update the firmware.
   - 我更新固件卡在死循环里了。
