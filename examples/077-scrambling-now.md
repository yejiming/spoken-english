# 077-scrambling-now Scrambling Now

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm scrambling to ... right now.
- 意思: 正在手忙脚乱地
- 用法: 用于表示你此刻正在慌忙赶着做某事。

## 例句

1. I'm scrambling to find my keys right now.
   - 我正在手忙脚乱地找钥匙。

2. I'm scrambling to get the kids ready right now.
   - 我正在手忙脚乱地给孩子准备出门。

3. I'm scrambling to finish getting dressed right now.
   - 我正在手忙脚乱地穿衣服。

4. I'm scrambling to pack my lunch right now.
   - 我正在手忙脚乱地装午饭。

5. I'm scrambling to catch the bus right now.
   - 我正在手忙脚乱地赶公交。
