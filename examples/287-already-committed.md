# 287-already-committed Already Committed

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I already committed to ...
- 意思: 已经答应了
- 用法: 用于表示你已经答应了别的安排。

## 例句

1. I already committed to helping them move.
   - 我已经答应帮他们搬家了。

2. I already committed to a work event.
   - 我已经答应参加公司活动了。

3. I already committed to dinner with my parents.
   - 我已经答应和爸妈吃饭了。

4. I already committed to coaching the game.
   - 我已经答应去当教练了。

5. I already committed to volunteering that day.
   - 我那天已经答应去做志愿者了。
