# 163-brought-up-in-meeting Brought Up In Meeting

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I brought up ... in the meeting.
- 意思: 在会上提出了
- 用法: 用于说你在会议中提到了某个话题。

## 例句

1. I brought up the staffing issue in the meeting.
   - 我在会上提出了人员配置的问题。

2. I brought up the timeline concern in the meeting.
   - 我在会上提出了对时间线的担忧。

3. I brought up the idea of remote work in the meeting.
   - 我在会上提出了远程办公的想法。

4. I brought up the customer feedback in the meeting.
   - 我在会上提出了客户反馈的问题。

5. I brought up the need for new equipment in the meeting.
   - 我在会上提出了需要新设备的问题。
