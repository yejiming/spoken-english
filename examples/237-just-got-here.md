# 237-just-got-here Just Got Here

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I just got to ...
- 意思: 刚刚到了
- 用法: 用于告诉别人你刚到某个地方。

## 例句

1. I just got to the restaurant.
   - 我刚到餐厅。

2. I just got to the parking lot.
   - 我刚到停车场。

3. I just got to the venue.
   - 我刚到活动场地。

4. I just got to Lisa's apartment.
   - 我刚到丽莎家。

5. I just got to the rooftop.
   - 我刚到天台。
