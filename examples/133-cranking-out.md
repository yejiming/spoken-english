# 133-cranking-out Cranking Out

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I'm cranking out ... as fast as I can.
- 意思: 在拼命赶工
- 用法: 用于说你在快速大量地完成某些事。

## 例句

1. I'm cranking out these reports as fast as I can.
   - 我在拼命赶这些报告。

2. I'm cranking out practice problems as fast as I can.
   - 我在拼命刷练习题。

3. I'm cranking out emails as fast as I can.
   - 我在拼命回邮件。

4. I'm cranking out the slides as fast as I can.
   - 我在拼命做幻灯片。

5. I'm cranking out code as fast as I can.
   - 我在拼命写代码。
