# 273-switching-spots Switching Spots

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: We're switching ... to a different spot.
- 意思: 换个地方
- 用法: 用于通知大家活动地点有变动。

## 例句

1. We're switching dinner to a different spot.
   - 我们晚餐换个地方。

2. We're switching the hangout to a different spot.
   - 我们聚会换个地方。

3. We're switching pregame to a different spot.
   - 我们预热换个地方。

4. We're switching the party to a different spot.
   - 我们派对换个地方。

5. We're switching brunch to a different spot.
   - 我们早午餐换个地方。
