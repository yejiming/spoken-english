# 85-really-into Really Into

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm really into ...
- 意思: 强烈喜欢。
- 用法: 用于说你很来电。

## 例句

1. I'm really into sharing honest feedback
   - 我真的很喜欢给出坦诚反馈。

2. I'm really into waiting too long
   - 我真的很喜欢等太久。

3. I'm really into the weather today
   - 我真的很喜欢今天的天气。

4. I'm really into speaking up in meetings
   - 我真的很喜欢在会议上发言。

5. I'm really into spending money on that
   - 我真的很喜欢为那个花钱。
