# 073-should-ve-earlier Should've Earlier

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I should've ... earlier.
- 意思: 早该做的
- 用法: 用于后悔没有更早去做某件事。

## 例句

1. I should've gone to bed earlier.
   - 我早该早点睡的。

2. I should've started cooking earlier.
   - 我早该早点开始做饭的。

3. I should've left the house earlier.
   - 我早该早点出门的。

4. I should've finished packing earlier.
   - 我早该早点收拾好行李的。

5. I should've set my alarm earlier.
   - 我早该早点定闹钟的。
