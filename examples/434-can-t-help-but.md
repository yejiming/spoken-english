# 434-can-t-help-but Can't Help But

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can't help but ...
- 意思: 忍不住
- 用法: 用于说控制不住地做某事。

## 例句

1. I can't help but check my phone every five minutes.
   - 我忍不住每五分钟看一次手机。

2. I can't help but laugh when I watch that clip.
   - 我看那个视频忍不住笑。

3. I can't help but worry about the presentation.
   - 我忍不住担心那个演讲。

4. I can't help but compare prices before buying anything.
   - 我买东西前忍不住比价。

5. I can't help but sing along to that song.
   - 我听那首歌忍不住跟着唱。
