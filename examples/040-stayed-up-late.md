# 040-stayed-up-late Stayed Up Late

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I stayed up late ...ing.
- 意思: 熬夜做了某事
- 用法: 用于描述昨晚熬夜做了什么事情。

## 例句

1. I stayed up late watching a drama.
   - 我熬夜看了一部剧。

2. I stayed up late reading.
   - 我熬夜看书了。

3. I stayed up late scrolling through social media.
   - 我熬夜刷社交媒体了。

4. I stayed up late gaming.
   - 我熬夜打游戏了。

5. I stayed up late chatting with friends.
   - 我熬夜跟朋友聊天了。
