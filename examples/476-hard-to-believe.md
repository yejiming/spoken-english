# 476-hard-to-believe Hard To Believe

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It's hard to believe ...
- 意思: 难以置信
- 用法: 用于说某事令人惊讶到不敢相信。

## 例句

1. It's hard to believe it's already been five years.
   - 五年都过去了真是难以置信。

2. It's hard to believe how fast technology is changing.
   - 科技变化这么快真是难以置信。

3. It's hard to believe I actually did it.
   - 我居然做到了真是难以置信。

4. It's hard to believe the semester is almost over.
   - 学期快结束了真是难以置信。

5. It's hard to believe how much the city has changed.
   - 这座城市变化这么大真是难以置信。
