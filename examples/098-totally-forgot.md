# 098-totally-forgot Totally Forgot

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I totally forgot about ...
- 意思: 完全忘了
- 用法: 用于表示你彻底把某件事忘得一干二净了。

## 例句

1. I totally forgot about my dentist appointment.
   - 我完全忘了牙医预约的事。

2. I totally forgot about the leftovers in the fridge.
   - 我完全忘了冰箱里的剩菜。

3. I totally forgot about returning that package.
   - 我完全忘了退那个快递。

4. I totally forgot about watering the plants.
   - 我完全忘了浇花的事。

5. I totally forgot about the laundry in the dryer.
   - 我完全忘了烘干机里还有衣服。
