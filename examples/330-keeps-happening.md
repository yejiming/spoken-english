# 330-keeps-happening Keeps Happening

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: This keeps happening every time I ...
- 意思: 每次都出问题
- 用法: 用于说某个问题反复出现。

## 例句

1. This keeps happening every time I open the app.
   - 每次打开应用都会出这个问题。

2. This keeps happening every time I try to print.
   - 每次打印都会出这个问题。

3. This keeps happening every time I plug in the USB.
   - 每次插USB都出这个问题。

4. This keeps happening every time I switch accounts.
   - 每次切换账号都出这个问题。

5. This keeps happening every time I use Bluetooth.
   - 每次用蓝牙都出这个问题。
