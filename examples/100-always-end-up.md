# 100-always-end-up Always End Up

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I always end up ... at night.
- 意思: 晚上总是会做
- 用法: 用于描述每到晚上你总会不由自主去做的事。

## 例句

1. I always end up snacking at night.
   - 我晚上总是忍不住吃零食。

2. I always end up scrolling through my phone at night.
   - 我晚上总是忍不住刷手机。

3. I always end up staying up too late at night.
   - 我晚上总是忍不住熬夜。

4. I always end up watching one more episode at night.
   - 我晚上总是忍不住再多看一集。

5. I always end up overthinking at night.
   - 我晚上总是忍不住想太多。
