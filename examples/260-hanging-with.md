# 260-hanging-with Hanging With

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm hanging with ... tonight.
- 意思: 今晚跟人玩
- 用法: 用于告诉别人你今晚要和谁一起。

## 例句

1. I'm hanging with Alex tonight.
   - 我今晚跟亚历克斯玩。

2. I'm hanging with the guys tonight.
   - 我今晚跟兄弟们玩。

3. I'm hanging with my sister tonight.
   - 我今晚和我姐待一起。

4. I'm hanging with some friends from school tonight.
   - 我今晚和学校朋友一起玩。

5. I'm hanging with my neighbor tonight.
   - 我今晚和邻居玩。
