# 427-sounds-right Sounds Right

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: That sounds about right for ...
- 意思: 差不多就是这样
- 用法: 用于说某说法基本准确。

## 例句

1. That sounds about right for the price of rent here.
   - 这里的房租差不多就是这个价。

2. That sounds about right for how long the commute takes.
   - 通勤时间差不多就是这么久。

3. That sounds about right for a weekend getaway budget.
   - 周末出游的预算差不多就是这样。

4. That sounds about right for the wait time at that place.
   - 那家店的等位时间差不多就这样。

5. That sounds about right for how much data we use.
   - 我们用的流量差不多就是这么多。
