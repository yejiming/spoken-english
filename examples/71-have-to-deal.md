# 71-have-to-deal Have To Deal

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I have to deal with ...
- 意思: 不得不处理麻烦。
- 用法: 用于说你必须面对。

## 例句

1. I have to deal with fix the microphone
   - 我得处理修好麦克风。

2. I have to deal with load the page
   - 我得处理加载页面。

3. I have to deal with open the attachment
   - 我得处理打开附件。

4. I have to deal with keep the meeting moving
   - 我得处理让会议继续推进。

5. I have to deal with use the old cable
   - 我得处理用那根旧电缆。
