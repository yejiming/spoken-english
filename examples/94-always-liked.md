# 94-always-liked Always Liked

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I've always liked ...
- 意思: 长期偏好。
- 用法: 用于说一直喜欢。

## 例句

1. I've always liked the way this feels
   - 我一直都喜欢这种感觉。

2. I've always liked staying home tonight
   - 我一直都喜欢今晚待在家。

3. I've always liked the new restaurant
   - 我一直都喜欢那家新餐厅。

4. I've always liked working from home
   - 我一直都喜欢在家办公。

5. I've always liked people being late
   - 我一直都喜欢别人迟到。
