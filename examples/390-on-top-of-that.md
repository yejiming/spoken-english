# 390-on-top-of-that On Top Of That

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: On top of everything, now ... is broken too.
- 意思: 雪上加霜
- 用法: 用于说已有问题上又出新问题。

## 例句

1. On top of everything, now the printer is broken too.
   - 雪上加霜，现在打印机也坏了。

2. On top of everything, now the hot water is broken too.
   - 雪上加霜，现在热水器也坏了。

3. On top of everything, now my phone screen is broken too.
   - 雪上加霜，现在手机屏幕也碎了。

4. On top of everything, now the garage door is broken too.
   - 雪上加霜，现在车库门也坏了。

5. On top of everything, now the backup drive is broken too.
   - 雪上加霜，现在备份硬盘也坏了。
