# 349-took-too-long Took Too Long

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I took too long on ...
- 意思: 花太长时间了
- 用法: 用于说在某件事上耽误了太久。

## 例句

1. I took too long on the first section of the test.
   - 我考试第一部分花太长时间了。

2. I took too long on choosing a design.
   - 我选设计方案花太长时间了。

3. I took too long on replying to that email.
   - 我回那封邮件花太长时间了。

4. I took too long on debugging that one issue.
   - 我修那个bug花太长时间了。

5. I took too long on making a decision.
   - 我做那个决定花太长时间了。
