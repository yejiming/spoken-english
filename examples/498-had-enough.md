# 498-had-enough Had Enough

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I've had enough of ...
- 意思: 受够了
- 用法: 用于说再也无法忍受某事。

## 例句

1. I've had enough of this endless paperwork.
   - 我受够了没完没了的文书工作。

2. I've had enough of people flaking on plans.
   - 我受够了别人放鸽子。

3. I've had enough of the terrible parking situation.
   - 我受够了糟糕的停车状况。

4. I've had enough of being stuck in meetings all day.
   - 我受够了一整天被困在会议里。

5. I've had enough of the bugs in this software.
   - 我受够了这个软件里的bug。
