# 448-let-down-by Let Down By

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I feel let down by ...
- 意思: 感觉被辜负了
- 用法: 用于说对某人或某事感到失望。

## 例句

1. I feel let down by the customer service experience.
   - 这次客服体验让我感觉被辜负了。

2. I feel let down by the movie after all the hype.
   - 那么多人吹的电影让我失望了。

3. I feel let down by the quality of the product.
   - 产品质量让我感觉被辜负了。

4. I feel let down by how the project turned out.
   - 项目的结果让我感觉被辜负了。

5. I feel let down by the restaurant's service.
   - 那家餐厅的服务让我感觉被辜负了。
