# 417-not-convinced Not Convinced

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm not convinced that ...
- 意思: 尚未被说服
- 用法: 用于表达对某种说法或想法的质疑。

## 例句

1. I'm not convinced that this plan will actually save us money.
   - 我还不太相信这个方案真的能帮我们省钱。

2. I'm not convinced that working longer hours improves productivity.
   - 我还不太相信加班能提高效率。

3. I'm not convinced that he's the right person for the role.
   - 我还不太相信他是这个岗位的合适人选。

4. I'm not convinced that moving offices is necessary right now.
   - 我还不太相信现在有必要搬办公室。

5. I'm not convinced that the new policy will make a difference.
   - 我还不太相信新政策会带来什么改变。
