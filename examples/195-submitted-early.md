# 195-submitted-early Submitted Early

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I submitted ... a day early.
- 意思: 提前一天交了
- 用法: 用于说你比截止日期提前一天就提交了。

## 例句

1. I submitted the application a day early.
   - 我提前一天交了申请。

2. I submitted my thesis draft a day early.
   - 我提前一天交了论文草稿。

3. I submitted the project deliverable a day early.
   - 我提前一天交了项目交付物。

4. I submitted the tax filing a day early.
   - 我提前一天交了税务申报。

5. I submitted the homework a day early.
   - 我提前一天交了作业。
