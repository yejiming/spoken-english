# 172-breezed-through Breezed Through

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I breezed through ...
- 意思: 轻松搞定了
- 用法: 用于说你很容易地完成了某件事。

## 例句

1. I breezed through the quiz.
   - 我轻轻松松做完了小测验。

2. I breezed through the onboarding paperwork.
   - 我轻松搞定了入职手续。

3. I breezed through the first three chapters.
   - 我轻松读完了前三章。

4. I breezed through the interview.
   - 我轻松通过了面试。

5. I breezed through the compliance checklist.
   - 我轻松搞定了合规检查清单。
