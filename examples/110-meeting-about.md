# 110-meeting-about Meeting About

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I've got to prepare ... for the meeting.
- 意思: 得为会议做准备
- 用法: 用于说你要为开会准备某些材料。

## 例句

1. I've got to prepare the agenda for the meeting.
   - 我得为开会准备议程。

2. I've got to prepare a status update for the meeting.
   - 我得为开会准备一份进展汇报。

3. I've got to prepare the sales figures for the meeting.
   - 我得为开会准备销售数据。

4. I've got to prepare a demo for the meeting.
   - 我得为开会准备一个演示。

5. I've got to prepare some talking points for the meeting.
   - 我得为开会准备一些要点。
