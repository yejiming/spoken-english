# 440-couldn-t-care-less Couldn't Care Less

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I couldn't care less about ...
- 意思: 完全不在乎
- 用法: 用于说对某事毫不关心。

## 例句

1. I couldn't care less about celebrity gossip.
   - 我完全不在乎明星八卦。

2. I couldn't care less about who wins the game tonight.
   - 我完全不在乎今晚谁赢比赛。

3. I couldn't care less about having the latest phone.
   - 我完全不在乎有没有最新手机。

4. I couldn't care less about social media followers.
   - 我完全不在乎社交媒体粉丝数。

5. I couldn't care less about brand names on clothes.
   - 我完全不在乎衣服上的品牌。
