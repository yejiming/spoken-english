# 387-way-off Way Off

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: My ... was way off.
- 意思: 差太远了
- 用法: 用于说估计或结果偏差很大。

## 例句

1. My estimate was way off on the project cost.
   - 我对项目成本的估计差太远了。

2. My calculation was way off on the measurements.
   - 我的尺寸计算差太远了。

3. My prediction was way off about the timeline.
   - 我对时间线的预测差太远了。

4. My first draft was way off from what they wanted.
   - 我的初稿跟他们要的差太远了。

5. My assumption was way off about the requirements.
   - 我对需求的假设差太远了。
