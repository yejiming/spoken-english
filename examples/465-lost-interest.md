# 465-lost-interest Lost Interest

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I've lost interest in ...
- 意思: 失去兴趣了
- 用法: 用于说对以前感兴趣的事不再关心。

## 例句

1. I've lost interest in scrolling social media.
   - 我对刷社交媒体失去兴趣了。

2. I've lost interest in following the news every day.
   - 我对每天看新闻失去兴趣了。

3. I've lost interest in that TV series after season two.
   - 第二季之后我对那部剧失去兴趣了。

4. I've lost interest in trying to impress people.
   - 我对试图讨好别人失去兴趣了。

5. I've lost interest in fast fashion.
   - 我对快时尚失去兴趣了。
