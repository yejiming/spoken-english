# 92-agree-with Agree With

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm not sure I agree with ...
- 意思: 保留意见。
- 用法: 用于表达不同看法。

## 例句

1. I'm not sure I agree with waiting too long
   - 我不太确定我同不同意等太久。

2. I'm not sure I agree with the weather today
   - 我不太确定我同不同意今天的天气。

3. I'm not sure I agree with speaking up in meetings
   - 我不太确定我同不同意在会议上发言。

4. I'm not sure I agree with spending money on that
   - 我不太确定我同不同意为那个花钱。

5. I'm not sure I agree with this kind of music
   - 我不太确定我同不同意这种音乐。
