# 382-caught-too-late Caught Too Late

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I caught ... too late.
- 意思: 发现太晚了
- 用法: 用于说发现问题时已经太迟。

## 例句

1. I caught the typo too late — it's already printed.
   - 我发现拼写错误太晚了，已经印了。

2. I caught the billing error too late.
   - 我发现账单错误太晚了。

3. I caught the wrong date on the invite too late.
   - 我发现邀请函上日期错了但太晚了。

4. I caught the bug too late — it's already in production.
   - 我发现bug太晚了，已经上线了。

5. I caught the expired coupon too late.
   - 我发现优惠券过期了但太晚了。
