# 318-avoiding Avoiding

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm trying to avoid ...
- 意思: 尽量想要避免
- 用法: 用于表达你在努力避免某种不好的结果。

## 例句

1. I'm trying to avoid making things worse by touching the wiring.
   - 我尽量不碰线路以免情况更糟。

2. I'm trying to avoid spending a fortune on repairs.
   - 我尽量避免花大价钱修理。

3. I'm trying to avoid losing any more data.
   - 我尽量避免再丢失更多数据。

4. I'm trying to avoid calling a technician if I can fix it myself.
   - 如果我能自己修好，就尽量不叫技术人员。

5. I'm trying to avoid disrupting the whole network.
   - 我尽量避免影响整个网络。
