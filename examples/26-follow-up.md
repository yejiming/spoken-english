# 26-follow-up Follow Up

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I have to follow up on ...
- 意思: 后续跟进。
- 用法: 用于说需要继续推进。

## 例句

1. I have to follow up on the handout
   - 我得跟进讲义。

2. I have to follow up on the summary
   - 我得跟进总结。

3. I have to follow up on my notes
   - 我得跟进我的笔记。

4. I have to follow up on the assignment
   - 我得跟进作业。

5. I have to follow up on the presentation
   - 我得跟进演示稿。
