# 249-still-on Still On

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Are we still on for ...?
- 意思: 还去不去
- 用法: 用于确认之前的约定是否还有效。

## 例句

1. Are we still on for Friday?
   - 周五还去不去？

2. Are we still on for the concert?
   - 演唱会还去吗？

3. Are we still on for brunch?
   - 早午餐还吃吗？

4. Are we still on for the hike?
   - 还去爬山不？

5. Are we still on for tonight?
   - 今晚还照常吗？
