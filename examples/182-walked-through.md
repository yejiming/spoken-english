# 182-walked-through Walked Through

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I walked through ... with my manager.
- 意思: 和经理过了一遍
- 用法: 用于说你和经理一起详细讨论了某件事。

## 例句

1. I walked through the project plan with my manager.
   - 我和经理过了一遍项目计划。

2. I walked through the quarterly numbers with my manager.
   - 我和经理过了一遍季度数据。

3. I walked through my career goals with my manager.
   - 我和经理聊了一下我的职业目标。

4. I walked through the incident report with my manager.
   - 我和经理过了一遍事故报告。

5. I walked through the new process with my manager.
   - 我和经理过了一遍新流程。
