# 034-halfway-through Halfway Through

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm halfway through ...
- 意思: 做到一半了
- 用法: 用于描述某件事完成了大约一半。

## 例句

1. I'm halfway through doing the laundry.
   - 我衣服洗到一半了。

2. I'm halfway through this book.
   - 这本书我看了一半了。

3. I'm halfway through cleaning the kitchen.
   - 厨房我打扫到一半了。

4. I'm halfway through my morning run.
   - 我晨跑跑了一半了。

5. I'm halfway through packing for the trip.
   - 行李我收拾到一半了。
