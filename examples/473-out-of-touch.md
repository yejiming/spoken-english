# 473-out-of-touch Out Of Touch

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I feel out of touch with ...
- 意思: 感觉脱节了
- 用法: 用于说感觉跟不上或脱离了某事。

## 例句

1. I feel out of touch with the latest tech trends.
   - 我感觉跟最新科技趋势脱节了。

2. I feel out of touch with pop music these days.
   - 我感觉跟现在的流行音乐脱节了。

3. I feel out of touch with my old college friends.
   - 我感觉跟大学老朋友们脱节了。

4. I feel out of touch with what's popular on social media.
   - 我感觉跟社交媒体上的热门脱节了。

5. I feel out of touch with the younger generation.
   - 我感觉跟年轻一代脱节了。
