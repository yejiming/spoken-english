# 412-agree-with Agree With

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm not sure I agree with ...
- 意思: 保留不同意见
- 用法: 用于礼貌地表达与他人或某观点的分歧。

## 例句

1. I'm not sure I agree with you on that point.
   - 在那一点上我不太同意你的看法。

2. I'm not sure I agree with the idea of cutting the budget.
   - 我不太同意削减预算的想法。

3. I'm not sure I agree with her approach to the problem.
   - 我不太同意她解决问题的方法。

4. I'm not sure I agree with the decision to postpone it.
   - 我不太同意推迟的那个决定。

5. I'm not sure I agree with what the article says.
   - 我不太同意那篇文章的说法。
