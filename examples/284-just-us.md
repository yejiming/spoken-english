# 284-just-us Just Us

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Is it just us or is ... coming too?
- 意思: 就咱俩吗
- 用法: 用于确认聚会的参加人数。

## 例句

1. Is it just us or is Sarah coming too?
   - 就咱俩还是莎拉也来？

2. Is it just us or is the whole group coming too?
   - 就咱俩还是大家都来？

3. Is it just us or is your boyfriend coming too?
   - 就咱俩还是你男朋友也来？

4. Is it just us or is Mike coming too?
   - 就咱俩还是迈克也来？

5. Is it just us or is anyone else coming too?
   - 就咱俩还是还有别人来？
