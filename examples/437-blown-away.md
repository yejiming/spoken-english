# 437-blown-away Blown Away

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I was blown away by ...
- 意思: 被震撼到了
- 用法: 用于说被某事深深打动或震撼。

## 例句

1. I was blown away by the sunset at the beach.
   - 海滩的日落把我震撼到了。

2. I was blown away by how good the food was.
   - 食物的味道把我震撼到了。

3. I was blown away by the concert last night.
   - 昨晚的演唱会把我震撼到了。

4. I was blown away by the quality of the documentary.
   - 那部纪录片的质量把我震撼到了。

5. I was blown away by how kind everyone was there.
   - 那里每个人的善意把我震撼到了。
