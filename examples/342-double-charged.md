# 342-double-charged Double Charged

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I got double-charged for ...
- 意思: 被重复扣费了
- 用法: 用于说同一笔费用被扣了两次。

## 例句

1. I got double-charged for my gym membership.
   - 健身房会员费被重复扣了。

2. I got double-charged for the concert tickets.
   - 演唱会门票被重复扣费了。

3. I got double-charged for my phone bill.
   - 手机话费被重复扣了。

4. I got double-charged for the grocery delivery.
   - 生鲜配送被重复扣费了。

5. I got double-charged for the streaming service.
   - 流媒体订阅被重复扣费了。
