# 138-handed-in Handed In

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I already handed in ...
- 意思: 已经交了
- 用法: 用于说你已经提交了某样东西。

## 例句

1. I already handed in my resignation letter.
   - 我已经交了辞职信了。

2. I already handed in the homework.
   - 我已经交了作业了。

3. I already handed in my part of the project.
   - 我已经交了我负责的那部分了。

4. I already handed in the expense report.
   - 我已经交了报销单了。

5. I already handed in the final draft.
   - 我已经交了终稿了。
