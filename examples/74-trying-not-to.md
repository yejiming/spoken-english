# 74-trying-not-to Trying Not To

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm trying not to ...
- 意思: 避免更糟。
- 用法: 用于克制自己。

## 例句

1. I'm trying not to get this sorted out
   - 我尽量不去把这件事处理好。

2. I'm trying not to open the app
   - 我尽量不去打开应用。

3. I'm trying not to log in
   - 我尽量不去登录。

4. I'm trying not to send the file
   - 我尽量不去发送文件。

5. I'm trying not to connect the printer
   - 我尽量不去连接打印机。
