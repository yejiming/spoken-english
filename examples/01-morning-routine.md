# 01-morning-routine Morning Routine

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I usually ... before breakfast.
- 意思: 早晨固定习惯。
- 用法: 用于描述每天早上的固定动作。

## 例句

1. I usually make coffee before breakfast.
   - 我通常会在早餐前泡咖啡。

2. I usually check my phone before breakfast.
   - 我通常会在早餐前看手机。

3. I usually take a shower before breakfast.
   - 我通常会在早餐前洗个澡。

4. I usually get dressed before breakfast.
   - 我通常会在早餐前穿好衣服。

5. I usually pack my bag before breakfast.
   - 我通常会在早餐前收拾书包。
