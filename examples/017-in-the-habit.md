# 017-in-the-habit In The Habit

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm in the habit of ...
- 意思: 固定习惯表达
- 用法: 用于描述长期养成的习惯。

## 例句

1. I'm in the habit of checking my email first thing.
   - 我有一起来就看邮件的习惯。

2. I'm in the habit of taking a walk after lunch.
   - 我有午饭后散步的习惯。

3. I'm in the habit of writing a to-do list every night.
   - 我有每晚写待办清单的习惯。

4. I'm in the habit of keeping my phone on silent.
   - 我有把手机调静音的习惯。

5. I'm in the habit of double-checking the door before bed.
   - 我有睡前再检查一遍门锁的习惯。
