# 364-can-t-access Can't Access

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I can't access ... right now.
- 意思: 现在进不去
- 用法: 用于说暂时无法访问某样东西。

## 例句

1. I can't access the shared folder right now.
   - 我现在进不去共享文件夹。

2. I can't access my email right now.
   - 我现在进不去邮箱。

3. I can't access the VPN right now.
   - 我现在连不上VPN。

4. I can't access the company dashboard right now.
   - 我现在进不去公司仪表盘。

5. I can't access the document from my phone right now.
   - 我现在手机上进不去那个文档。
