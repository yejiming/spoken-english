# 019-before-i-leave Before I Leave

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I have to ... before I leave.
- 意思: 出门前必须做
- 用法: 用于说离开前的必要动作。

## 例句

1. I have to feed the cat before I leave.
   - 我走之前得先喂猫。

2. I have to lock all the windows before I leave.
   - 我走之前得先把窗户都锁好。

3. I have to take out the trash before I leave.
   - 我走之前得先把垃圾倒了。

4. I have to unplug the iron before I leave.
   - 我走之前得先把熨斗拔了。

5. I have to water the plants before I leave.
   - 我走之前得先给花浇水。
