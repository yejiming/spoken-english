# 080-taking-my-time Taking My Time

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm taking my time with ...
- 意思: 慢慢来不着急
- 用法: 用于表示你在不紧不慢地做某件事。

## 例句

1. I'm taking my time with breakfast today.
   - 我今天慢慢享用早餐。

2. I'm taking my time with getting ready.
   - 我慢慢准备不着急。

3. I'm taking my time with my morning coffee.
   - 我慢慢品着早咖啡。

4. I'm taking my time with this book.
   - 我慢慢看这本书不赶进度。

5. I'm taking my time with the house cleaning.
   - 我慢慢打扫不着急。
