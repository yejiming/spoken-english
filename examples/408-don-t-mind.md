# 408-don-t-mind Don't Mind

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I don't mind ...
- 意思: 不介意
- 用法: 用于表达对某事的接受或无所谓。

## 例句

1. I don't mind working late if the project needs it.
   - 如果项目需要，我不介意加班。

2. I don't mind cooking for everyone.
   - 我不介意给大家做饭。

3. I don't mind waiting a little longer.
   - 我不介意多等一会儿。

4. I don't mind sitting in the back row.
   - 我不介意坐在最后一排。

5. I don't mind taking the early flight.
   - 我不介意坐早班飞机。
