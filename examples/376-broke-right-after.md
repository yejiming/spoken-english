# 376-broke-right-after Broke Right After

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: It broke again right after I fixed ...
- 意思: 刚修好又坏了
- 用法: 用于说刚修好的东西立刻又坏了。

## 例句

1. It broke again right after I fixed the screen.
   - 屏幕刚修好又坏了。

2. It broke again right after I fixed the faucet.
   - 水龙头刚修好又坏了。

3. It broke again right after I fixed the door handle.
   - 门把手刚修好又坏了。

4. It broke again right after I fixed the headlight.
   - 车灯刚修好又坏了。

5. It broke again right after I fixed the Wi-Fi setup.
   - Wi-Fi设置刚弄好又坏了。
