# 356-delayed-by Delayed By

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I got delayed by ...
- 意思: 被耽搁了
- 用法: 用于说被某件事耽搁了时间。

## 例句

1. I got delayed by a traffic jam on the freeway.
   - 我被高速上的堵车耽搁了。

2. I got delayed by a last-minute meeting.
   - 我被一个临时会议耽搁了。

3. I got delayed by a long phone call.
   - 我被一通很长的电话耽搁了。

4. I got delayed by the security check at the airport.
   - 我被机场安检耽搁了。

5. I got delayed by a problem with my car.
   - 我被车出的问题耽搁了。
