# 474-miss-the-days Miss The Days

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I miss the days when ...
- 意思: 怀念那时候
- 用法: 用于表达对过去某段时光的怀念。

## 例句

1. I miss the days when weekends felt longer.
   - 我怀念那时候周末感觉更长。

2. I miss the days when we used to hang out every Friday.
   - 我怀念那时候我们每周五一起玩。

3. I miss the days when things were simpler.
   - 我怀念那时候事情更简单。

4. I miss the days when I had no responsibilities.
   - 我怀念那时候没有责任。

5. I miss the days when we'd just drive around with no plan.
   - 我怀念那时候我们漫无目的地开车兜风。
