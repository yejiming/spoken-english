# 477-wish-i-could Wish I Could

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I wish I could ...
- 意思: 真希望我能
- 用法: 用于表达一个无法实现的愿望。

## 例句

1. I wish I could travel more often.
   - 真希望我能多旅行。

2. I wish I could play the piano.
   - 真希望我能弹钢琴。

3. I wish I could go back and redo that conversation.
   - 真希望我能回去重来那次对话。

4. I wish I could take a month off just to read.
   - 真希望我能休一个月只看书。

5. I wish I could speak three languages fluently.
   - 真希望我能流利地说三种语言。
