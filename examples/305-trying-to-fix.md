# 305-trying-to-fix Trying To Fix

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm trying to fix ...
- 意思: 正在尝试修好
- 用法: 用于表达你正在动手修某个东西。

## 例句

1. I'm trying to fix the leaky faucet in the bathroom.
   - 我在试着修浴室漏水的水龙头。

2. I'm trying to fix the broken zipper on my bag.
   - 我在试着修包上坏掉的拉链。

3. I'm trying to fix the connection issue with the speaker.
   - 我在试着修音箱的连接问题。

4. I'm trying to fix the dent on my car door.
   - 我在试着修车门上的凹痕。

5. I'm trying to fix the error in the spreadsheet.
   - 我在试着修表格里的错误。
