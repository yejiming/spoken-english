# 054-swinging-by Swinging By

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm swinging by ... real quick.
- 意思: 顺道过去一趟
- 用法: 用于告诉别人你要快速地去某个地方一趟。

## 例句

1. I'm swinging by the pharmacy real quick.
   - 我顺道去趟药店。

2. I'm swinging by the bank real quick.
   - 我顺道去趟银行。

3. I'm swinging by the dry cleaner's real quick.
   - 我顺道去趟干洗店。

4. I'm swinging by my friend's place real quick.
   - 我顺道去趟朋友家。

5. I'm swinging by the convenience store real quick.
   - 我顺道去趟便利店。
