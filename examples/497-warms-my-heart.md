# 497-warms-my-heart Warms My Heart

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It warms my heart when ...
- 意思: 让我心里暖暖的
- 用法: 用于说某事让你感到温暖感动。

## 例句

1. It warms my heart when kids help each other out.
   - 小朋友互相帮忙让我心里暖暖的。

2. It warms my heart when someone holds the door for me.
   - 有人帮我扶门让我心里暖暖的。

3. It warms my heart when my friend checks in on me.
   - 朋友来问候我让我心里暖暖的。

4. It warms my heart when I see families playing in the park.
   - 看到家人们在公园玩让我心里暖暖的。

5. It warms my heart when strangers are kind to each other.
   - 陌生人之间善意相待让我心里暖暖的。
