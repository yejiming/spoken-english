# 443-makes-me-think Makes Me Think

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It makes me think about ...
- 意思: 让我开始思考
- 用法: 用于说某事引发了你的思考。

## 例句

1. It makes me think about how fast time goes by.
   - 这让我开始思考时间过得多快。

2. It makes me think about what I really want in life.
   - 这让我开始思考我到底想要什么。

3. It makes me think about how different things were.
   - 这让我开始思考以前有多不一样。

4. It makes me think about my priorities.
   - 这让我开始思考我的优先级。

5. It makes me think about the impact of small choices.
   - 这让我开始思考小选择的影响。
