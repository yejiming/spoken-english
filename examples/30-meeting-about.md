# 30-meeting-about Meeting About

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I've got to prepare ... for the meeting.
- 意思: 会议前要准备。
- 用法: 用于说会前准备内容。

## 例句

1. I've got to prepare the draft for the meeting.
   - 我得为了会议准备草稿。

2. I've got to prepare the call setup for the meeting.
   - 我得为了会议准备电话会议安排。

3. I've got to prepare the client follow-up for the meeting.
   - 我得为了会议准备客户跟进。

4. I've got to prepare the deadline for the meeting.
   - 我得为了会议准备截止时间。

5. I've got to prepare my files for the meeting.
   - 我得为了会议准备我的文件。
