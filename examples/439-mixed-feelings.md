# 439-mixed-feelings Mixed Feelings

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I have mixed feelings about ...
- 意思: 心情很复杂
- 用法: 用于说对某事既有正面也有负面感受。

## 例句

1. I have mixed feelings about the new office layout.
   - 对新的办公室布局我心情很复杂。

2. I have mixed feelings about graduating.
   - 对毕业我心情很复杂。

3. I have mixed feelings about moving to a new city.
   - 对搬去新城市我心情很复杂。

4. I have mixed feelings about my friend's new partner.
   - 对朋友的新对象我心情很复杂。

5. I have mixed feelings about the ending of the book.
   - 对那本书的结局我心情很复杂。
