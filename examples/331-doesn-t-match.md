# 331-doesn-t-match Doesn't Match

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: The ... doesn't match what I expected.
- 意思: 跟预期不符
- 用法: 用于说结果和预期不一致。

## 例句

1. The color doesn't match what I expected from the website.
   - 颜色跟网站上看到的不一样。

2. The bill doesn't match what I expected at all.
   - 账单跟我预期的完全不符。

3. The delivery time doesn't match what I expected.
   - 配送时间跟我预期的不一样。

4. The product quality doesn't match what I expected.
   - 产品质量跟我预期的不符。

5. The final report doesn't match what I expected.
   - 最终报告跟我预期的不一样。
