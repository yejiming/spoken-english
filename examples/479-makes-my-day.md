# 479-makes-my-day Makes My Day

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It makes my day when ...
- 意思: 让我一天都开心
- 用法: 用于说某事让你心情大好。

## 例句

1. It makes my day when my dog greets me at the door.
   - 狗狗在门口迎接我让我一天都开心。

2. It makes my day when a stranger smiles at me.
   - 陌生人对我微笑让我一天都开心。

3. It makes my day when I find a good parking spot.
   - 找到好车位让我一天都开心。

4. It makes my day when someone compliments my work.
   - 有人夸我的工作让我一天都开心。

5. It makes my day when the coffee hits just right.
   - 咖啡味道刚刚好让我一天都开心。
