# 353-keeps-disconnecting Keeps Disconnecting

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: My ... keeps disconnecting.
- 意思: 老是断连
- 用法: 用于说设备频繁断开连接。

## 例句

1. My Bluetooth headphones keep disconnecting.
   - 我的蓝牙耳机老是断连。

2. My Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting every few minutes.
   - 我的Wi-Fi每隔几分钟就断连。

3. My external monitor keeps disconnecting.
   - 我的外接显示器老是断连。

4. My VPN keeps disconnecting during calls.
   - 打电话时我的VPN老是断连。

5. My wireless mouse keeps disconnecting randomly.
   - 我的无线鼠标老是随机断连。
