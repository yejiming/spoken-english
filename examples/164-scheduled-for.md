# 164-scheduled-for Scheduled For

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I'm scheduled for ... at three.
- 意思: 三点有安排
- 用法: 用于说你在某个时间有一项日程安排。

## 例句

1. I'm scheduled for a one-on-one at three.
   - 我三点有一个一对一会议。

2. I'm scheduled for a dentist appointment at three.
   - 我三点有个牙医预约。

3. I'm scheduled for a phone interview at three.
   - 我三点有一个电话面试。

4. I'm scheduled for a training session at three.
   - 我三点有一场培训。

5. I'm scheduled for a site visit at three.
   - 我三点有一个现场参观。
