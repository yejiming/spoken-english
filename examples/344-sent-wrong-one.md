# 344-sent-wrong-one Sent Wrong One

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I sent the wrong ... by accident.
- 意思: 发错了
- 用法: 用于说不小心发错了东西。

## 例句

1. I sent the wrong file by accident to my boss.
   - 我不小心把文件发错给老板了。

2. I sent the wrong email by accident to the whole team.
   - 我不小心把邮件发错给全组了。

3. I sent the wrong photo by accident in the group chat.
   - 我不小心在群里发错了照片。

4. I sent the wrong address by accident to the driver.
   - 我不小心发错了地址给司机。

5. I sent the wrong version by accident to the client.
   - 我不小心把错误版本发给客户了。
