# 441-not-my-thing Not My Thing

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: That's not really my thing — I'd rather ...
- 意思: 不是我的菜
- 用法: 用于说不感兴趣并表达偏好。

## 例句

1. That's not really my thing — I'd rather stay in and read.
   - 那不是我的菜，我宁可在家看书。

2. That's not really my thing — I'd rather go hiking.
   - 那不是我的菜，我更想去爬山。

3. That's not really my thing — I'd rather watch a documentary.
   - 那不是我的菜，我更想看纪录片。

4. That's not really my thing — I'd rather cook at home.
   - 那不是我的菜，我更想在家做饭。

5. That's not really my thing — I'd rather try something low-key.
   - 那不是我的菜，我更想做点安静的事。
