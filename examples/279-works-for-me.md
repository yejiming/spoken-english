# 279-works-for-me Works For Me

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Any time after ... works for me.
- 意思: 之后都行
- 用法: 用于告知对方你某个时间之后都有空。

## 例句

1. Any time after five works for me.
   - 五点之后我都行。

2. Any time after lunch works for me.
   - 午饭后我都行。

3. Any time after my meeting works for me.
   - 开完会后我都行。

4. Any time after three works for me.
   - 三点之后我都行。

5. Any time after the gym works for me.
   - 健身完后我都行。
