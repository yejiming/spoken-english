# 466-couldn-t-agree-more Couldn't Agree More

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I couldn't agree more about ...
- 意思: 再同意不过了
- 用法: 用于表示完全同意某观点。

## 例句

1. I couldn't agree more about needing better public transit.
   - 要改善公共交通我再同意不过了。

2. I couldn't agree more about the food at that place.
   - 那家店的食物我再同意不过了。

3. I couldn't agree more about taking mental health days.
   - 要休心理健康假我再同意不过了。

4. I couldn't agree more about prioritizing sleep.
   - 要重视睡眠我再同意不过了。

5. I couldn't agree more about the importance of reading.
   - 阅读的重要性我再同意不过了。
