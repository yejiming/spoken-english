# 416-impressed-by Impressed By

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm impressed by ...
- 意思: 印象深刻
- 用法: 用于对某人的表现或成就表达赞叹。

## 例句

1. I'm impressed by how quickly she picked up the new system.
   - 她这么快就上手了新系统，让我印象深刻。

2. I'm impressed by the quality of his presentation.
   - 他演示文稿的质量让我印象深刻。

3. I'm impressed by how much the team accomplished this week.
   - 团队这周完成的工作量让我印象深刻。

4. I'm impressed by the design of this new building.
   - 这栋新建筑的设计让我印象深刻。

5. I'm impressed by her dedication to the project.
   - 她对项目的投入让我印象深刻。
