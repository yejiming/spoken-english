# 202-want-to-tonight Want To Tonight

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Do you want to ... tonight?
- 意思: 约今晚一起做
- 用法: 用于邀请对方今晚参加某项活动。

## 例句

1. Do you want to try that new restaurant tonight?
   - 你今晚想去试那家新餐厅吗？

2. Do you want to watch a movie tonight?
   - 你今晚想看电影吗？

3. Do you want to go bowling tonight?
   - 你今晚想去打保龄球吗？

4. Do you want to cook something together tonight?
   - 你今晚想一起做饭吗？

5. Do you want to hang out at my place tonight?
   - 你今晚想来我家坐坐吗？
