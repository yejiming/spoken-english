# 04-been-meaning-to Been Meaning To

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I've been meaning to ...
- 意思: 想做很久但还没做。
- 用法: 用于说你惦记了很久。

## 例句

1. I've been meaning to walk the dog
   - 我一直想要遛狗。

2. I've been meaning to call my parents
   - 我一直想要给父母打电话。

3. I've been meaning to pay a few bills
   - 我一直想要付几笔账单。

4. I've been meaning to take out the trash
   - 我一直想要倒垃圾。

5. I've been meaning to open the windows
   - 我一直想要打开窗户。
