# 486-doesn-t-sit-right Doesn't Sit Right

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It doesn't sit right with me that ...
- 意思: 让我心里不舒服
- 用法: 用于说某事让你感到不对劲。

## 例句

1. It doesn't sit right with me that they got away with it.
   - 他们就这么逃脱了让我心里不舒服。

2. It doesn't sit right with me that the prices jumped so much.
   - 价格涨这么多让我心里不舒服。

3. It doesn't sit right with me that nobody spoke up.
   - 没人说话让我心里不舒服。

4. It doesn't sit right with me that the decision was made without us.
   - 没有我们参与就做了决定让我心里不舒服。

5. It doesn't sit right with me that they changed the rules mid-game.
   - 比赛中途改规则让我心里不舒服。
