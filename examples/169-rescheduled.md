# 169-rescheduled Rescheduled

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I rescheduled ... to next week.
- 意思: 改期到下周了
- 用法: 用于说你把某个安排推迟到了下周。

## 例句

1. I rescheduled the client call to next week.
   - 我把客户电话改到下周了。

2. I rescheduled the team lunch to next week.
   - 我把团队聚餐改到下周了。

3. I rescheduled my dentist appointment to next week.
   - 我把牙医预约改到下周了。

4. I rescheduled the review meeting to next week.
   - 我把评审会改到下周了。

5. I rescheduled the demo to next week.
   - 我把演示改到下周了。
