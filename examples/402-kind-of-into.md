# 402-kind-of-into Kind Of Into

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm kind of into ...
- 意思: 有一点喜欢
- 用法: 用于表达不太强烈的喜好。

## 例句

1. I'm kind of into jazz music lately.
   - 我最近有点喜欢爵士乐。

2. I'm kind of into cooking at home these days.
   - 我这段时间有点喜欢在家做饭。

3. I'm kind of into that new show everyone's watching.
   - 我有点喜欢大家都在追的那部新剧。

4. I'm kind of into hiking on weekends.
   - 我有点喜欢周末去爬山。

5. I'm kind of into photography as a hobby.
   - 我有点喜欢摄影这个爱好。
