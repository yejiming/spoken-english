# 355-everything-backed-up Everything Backed Up

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: Everything's backed up because of ...
- 意思: 全都积压了
- 用法: 用于说因为某个原因导致事情积压。

## 例句

1. Everything's backed up because of the system outage.
   - 系统宕机导致所有事都积压了。

2. Everything's backed up because of the holiday rush.
   - 节假日高峰导致全部积压了。

3. Everything's backed up because of one missing approval.
   - 因为少一个审批导致全部积压了。

4. Everything's backed up because of the shipping delay.
   - 物流延迟导致所有订单积压了。

5. Everything's backed up because of staff shortages.
   - 人手不够导致所有事都积压了。
