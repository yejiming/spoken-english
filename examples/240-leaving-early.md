# 240-leaving-early Leaving Early

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm leaving ... early tonight.
- 意思: 今晚先走
- 用法: 用于提前告知大家你会早点离开。

## 例句

1. I'm leaving the party early tonight.
   - 我今晚派对会先走。

2. I'm leaving dinner early tonight.
   - 我今晚晚餐会先走。

3. I'm leaving the game early tonight.
   - 我今晚比赛会先走。

4. I'm leaving the event early tonight.
   - 我今晚活动会先走。

5. I'm leaving the show early tonight.
   - 我今晚演出会先走。
