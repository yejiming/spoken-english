# 334-can-t-get-through Can't Get Through

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I can't get through to ...
- 意思: 联系不上
- 用法: 用于说怎么都联系不上对方。

## 例句

1. I can't get through to the customer service line.
   - 我打不通客服电话。

2. I can't get through to my insurance company.
   - 我联系不上保险公司。

3. I can't get through to the landlord about the leak.
   - 我联系不上房东说漏水的事。

4. I can't get through to tech support at all.
   - 我完全联系不上技术支持。

5. I can't get through to anyone at the airline.
   - 我联系不上航空公司任何人。
