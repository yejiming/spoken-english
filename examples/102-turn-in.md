# 102-turn-in Turn In

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I need to turn in ... today.
- 意思: 今天要交东西
- 用法: 用于说你需要在今天提交某份作业或文件。

## 例句

1. I need to turn in my homework today.
   - 我今天得交作业。

2. I need to turn in the expense report today.
   - 我今天得交费用报告。

3. I need to turn in my research paper today.
   - 我今天得交研究论文。

4. I need to turn in the signed contract today.
   - 我今天得交签好的合同。

5. I need to turn in my lab report today.
   - 我今天得交实验报告。
