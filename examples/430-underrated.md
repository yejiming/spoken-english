# 430-underrated Underrated

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I think ... is underrated.
- 意思: 觉得被低估了
- 用法: 用于说觉得某事物没得到应有关注。

## 例句

1. I think public libraries are underrated.
   - 我觉得公共图书馆被低估了。

2. I think napping is underrated.
   - 我觉得午睡被低估了。

3. I think handwritten notes are underrated.
   - 我觉得手写笔记被低估了。

4. I think walking meetings are underrated.
   - 我觉得散步开会被低估了。

5. I think leftovers for breakfast are underrated.
   - 我觉得早上吃剩菜被低估了。
