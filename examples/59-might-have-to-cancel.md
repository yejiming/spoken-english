# 59-might-have-to-cancel Might Have To Cancel

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I might have to cancel if I can't make ...
- 意思: 有变数时说。
- 用法: 用于保留取消可能。

## 例句

1. I might have to cancel if I can't make stay in tonight
   - 如果我不能赶上今晚待在家，我可能得取消。

2. I might have to cancel if I can't make head to the airport
   - 如果我不能赶上去机场，我可能得取消。

3. I might have to cancel if I can't make join us after work
   - 如果我不能赶上下班后加入我们，我可能得取消。

4. I might have to cancel if I can't make split the bill
   - 如果我不能赶上平摊账单，我可能得取消。

5. I might have to cancel if I can't make grab drinks
   - 如果我不能赶上喝点东西，我可能得取消。
