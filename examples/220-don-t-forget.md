# 220-don-t-forget Don't Forget

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Let's not forget to ...
- 意思: 别忘了要做
- 用法: 用于提醒大家不要忘记做某件事。

## 例句

1. Let's not forget to bring some snacks.
   - 别忘了带点零食。

2. Let's not forget to charge our phones.
   - 别忘了给手机充电。

3. Let's not forget to book a table ahead of time.
   - 别忘了提前订位。

4. Let's not forget to pick up the cake on the way.
   - 别忘了路上把蛋糕取了。

5. Let's not forget to invite Mike this time.
   - 这次别忘了叫上Mike。
