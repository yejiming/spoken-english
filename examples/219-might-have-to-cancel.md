# 219-might-have-to-cancel Might Have To Cancel

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I might have to cancel if I can't make ...
- 意思: 赶不上可能取消
- 用法: 用于表达如果赶不上某个活动可能需要取消。

## 例句

1. I might have to cancel if I can't make the seven o'clock show.
   - 如果赶不上七点的场，我可能得取消。

2. I might have to cancel if I can't make the dinner reservation.
   - 如果赶不上订好的晚餐，我可能得取消。

3. I might have to cancel if I can't make the morning flight.
   - 如果赶不上早班飞机，我可能得取消。

4. I might have to cancel if I can't make the team gathering.
   - 如果去不了团队聚会，我可能得取消。

5. I might have to cancel if I can't make the birthday party.
   - 如果去不了生日派对，我可能得取消。
