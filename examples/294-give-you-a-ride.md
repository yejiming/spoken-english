# 294-give-you-a-ride Give You A Ride

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I can give you a ride to ...
- 意思: 我送你去
- 用法: 用于主动提出开车送别人。

## 例句

1. I can give you a ride to the airport.
   - 我可以送你去机场。

2. I can give you a ride to the venue.
   - 我可以送你去场地。

3. I can give you a ride to the party.
   - 我可以送你去派对。

4. I can give you a ride to the restaurant.
   - 我可以送你去餐厅。

5. I can give you a ride to the station.
   - 我可以送你去车站。
