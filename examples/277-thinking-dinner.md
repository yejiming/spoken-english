# 277-thinking-dinner Thinking Dinner

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I was thinking dinner at ...
- 意思: 想去某地吃饭
- 用法: 用于提议在某个地方吃晚饭。

## 例句

1. I was thinking dinner at that Italian place.
   - 我想去那家意大利餐厅吃晚饭。

2. I was thinking dinner at the new steakhouse.
   - 我想去那家新牛排馆吃晚饭。

3. I was thinking dinner at the rooftop spot.
   - 我想去那个天台餐厅吃晚饭。

4. I was thinking dinner at seven.
   - 我想七点吃晚饭。

5. I was thinking dinner at your favorite place.
   - 我想去你最喜欢的那家吃晚饭。
