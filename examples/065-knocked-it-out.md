# 065-knocked-it-out Knocked It Out

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I knocked out ... early today.
- 意思: 今天提前搞定了
- 用法: 用于表示你高效地提前完成了某件事。

## 例句

1. I knocked out the grocery shopping early today.
   - 我今天提前把菜买好了。

2. I knocked out my morning chores early today.
   - 我今天提前把早上的家务干完了。

3. I knocked out all the laundry early today.
   - 我今天提前把衣服全洗了。

4. I knocked out the meal prep early today.
   - 我今天提前把饭菜备好了。

5. I knocked out the house cleaning early today.
   - 我今天提前把家打扫完了。
