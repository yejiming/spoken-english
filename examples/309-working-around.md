# 309-working-around Working Around

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm working around ...
- 意思: 在想办法绕过
- 用法: 用于表达你在绕开某个限制或故障。

## 例句

1. I'm working around a bug in the system.
   - 我在绕过系统里的一个bug。

2. I'm working around the broken elevator by taking the stairs.
   - 电梯坏了，我走楼梯绕过去。

3. I'm working around the storage limit by compressing files.
   - 我在压缩文件来绕过存储限制。

4. I'm working around the scheduling conflict.
   - 我在想办法绕过时间冲突。

5. I'm working around the network restriction.
   - 我在想办法绕过网络限制。
