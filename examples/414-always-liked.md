# 414-always-liked Always Liked

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I've always liked ...
- 意思: 一直喜欢
- 用法: 用于表达长久以来的偏好。

## 例句

1. I've always liked the sound of rain on the window.
   - 我一直都喜欢雨打窗户的声音。

2. I've always liked walking through the park in autumn.
   - 我一直都喜欢秋天在公园里散步。

3. I've always liked trying new restaurants.
   - 我一直都喜欢尝试新餐厅。

4. I've always liked working with my hands.
   - 我一直都喜欢做手工活。

5. I've always liked quiet mornings with a cup of coffee.
   - 我一直都喜欢安静的早晨配一杯咖啡。
