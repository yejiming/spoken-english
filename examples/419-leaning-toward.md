# 419-leaning-toward Leaning Toward

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm leaning toward ...
- 意思: 倾向于某选择
- 用法: 用于表达尚未最终决定但有偏好。

## 例句

1. I'm leaning toward taking the job offer in Shanghai.
   - 我比较倾向于接受上海的那个工作机会。

2. I'm leaning toward the blue one — it looks better.
   - 我比较倾向于蓝色那个——看起来更好。

3. I'm leaning toward staying home this holiday.
   - 我比较倾向于这个假期待在家。

4. I'm leaning toward signing up for the morning class.
   - 我比较倾向于报早上的课。

5. I'm leaning toward ordering the pasta instead.
   - 我比较倾向于改点意面。
