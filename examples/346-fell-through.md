# 346-fell-through Fell Through

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: The ... fell through at the last minute.
- 意思: 最后关头黄了
- 用法: 用于说计划在最后时刻泡汤了。

## 例句

1. The deal fell through at the last minute.
   - 这笔交易最后关头黄了。

2. The apartment fell through at the last minute.
   - 租房的事最后关头黄了。

3. The job offer fell through at the last minute.
   - 工作机会最后关头黄了。

4. The partnership fell through at the last minute.
   - 合作最后关头黄了。

5. The travel plan fell through at the last minute.
   - 旅行计划最后关头黄了。
