# 158-pushed-back Pushed Back

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: They pushed back the deadline for ...
- 意思: 截止日期推迟了
- 用法: 用于说某件事的截止日期被延后了。

## 例句

1. They pushed back the deadline for the project.
   - 项目的截止日期推迟了。

2. They pushed back the deadline for the final paper.
   - 期末论文的截止日期推迟了。

3. They pushed back the deadline for the application.
   - 申请的截止日期推迟了。

4. They pushed back the deadline for the product launch.
   - 产品发布的截止日期推迟了。

5. They pushed back the deadline for the code review.
   - 代码审查的截止日期推迟了。
