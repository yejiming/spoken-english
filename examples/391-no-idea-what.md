# 391-no-idea-what No Idea What

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I have no idea what went wrong with ...
- 意思: 完全不知道怎么了
- 用法: 用于说完全搞不清楚问题出在哪。

## 例句

1. I have no idea what went wrong with the install.
   - 我完全不知道安装哪里出了问题。

2. I have no idea what went wrong with the payment.
   - 我完全不知道付款哪里出了问题。

3. I have no idea what went wrong with the recipe.
   - 我完全不知道菜谱哪里出了问题。

4. I have no idea what went wrong with the connection.
   - 我完全不知道连接哪里出了问题。

5. I have no idea what went wrong with the migration.
   - 我完全不知道迁移哪里出了问题。
