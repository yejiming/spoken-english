# 203-thinking-of Thinking Of

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I was thinking we could ...
- 意思: 委婉提出建议
- 用法: 用于礼貌地提出一个想法或建议。

## 例句

1. I was thinking we could check out that new café downtown.
   - 我在想我们可以去看看市中心那家新咖啡馆。

2. I was thinking we could do a picnic this Saturday.
   - 我在想我们这周六可以去野餐。

3. I was thinking we could invite a few more people.
   - 我在想我们可以多叫几个人。

4. I was thinking we could try somewhere different this time.
   - 我在想这次我们可以换个地方试试。

5. I was thinking we could leave a bit earlier to beat the traffic.
   - 我在想我们可以早点出发避开堵车。
