# 067-every-single-day Every Single Day

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I go through ... every single day.
- 意思: 每天都要经历
- 用法: 用于描述每天都会重复经历的事情或流程。

## 例句

1. I go through the same routine every single day.
   - 我每天都重复同样的流程。

2. I go through so much coffee every single day.
   - 我每天都喝掉好多咖啡。

3. I go through this traffic every single day.
   - 我每天都要经历这种堵车。

4. I go through a ton of emails every single day.
   - 我每天都要处理一大堆邮件。

5. I go through the same struggle every single day.
   - 我每天都要经历同样的挣扎。
