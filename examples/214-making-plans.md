# 214-making-plans Making Plans

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm making plans to ...
- 意思: 在安排准备做
- 用法: 用于表达你正在筹划某项活动。

## 例句

1. I'm making plans to visit my sister next month.
   - 我在安排下个月去看我姐。

2. I'm making plans to throw a housewarming party.
   - 我在安排办一场乔迁派对。

3. I'm making plans to take a weekend trip.
   - 我在安排一趟周末短途旅行。

4. I'm making plans to meet up with my college friends.
   - 我在安排和大学朋友聚一聚。

5. I'm making plans to celebrate her birthday.
   - 我在安排怎么给她庆生。
