# 339-keeps-freezing Keeps Freezing

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: My ... keeps freezing up.
- 意思: 老是卡住
- 用法: 用于说设备或软件频繁卡住。

## 例句

1. My computer keeps freezing up when I multitask.
   - 我一开多任务电脑就卡住。

2. My phone keeps freezing up during calls.
   - 我打电话时手机老是卡住。

3. My streaming app keeps freezing up mid-show.
   - 看剧看到一半流媒体应用老卡。

4. My GPS keeps freezing up when I need it most.
   - 最需要导航的时候GPS老是卡住。

5. My smart TV keeps freezing up every evening.
   - 我的智能电视每晚都会卡住。
