# 433-sick-of Sick Of

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm sick of ...
- 意思: 受够了
- 用法: 用于说对某事感到厌烦透顶。

## 例句

1. I'm sick of cooking every single night.
   - 我受够了每晚都做饭。

2. I'm sick of getting spam emails all day.
   - 我受够了一整天收垃圾邮件。

3. I'm sick of this same commute every morning.
   - 我受够了每天早上同样的通勤。

4. I'm sick of dealing with slow customer service.
   - 我受够了慢吞吞的客服。

5. I'm sick of hearing the same excuses.
   - 我受够了听同样的借口。
