# 230-not-feeling-it Not Feeling It

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm not really feeling ... tonight.
- 意思: 今晚没兴致
- 用法: 用于委婉表示对某个安排没什么兴趣。

## 例句

1. I'm not really feeling the club tonight.
   - 我今晚不太想去夜店。

2. I'm not really feeling a big dinner tonight.
   - 我今晚不太想吃大餐。

3. I'm not really feeling the party tonight.
   - 我今晚不太想去派对。

4. I'm not really feeling bowling tonight.
   - 我今晚不太想去打保龄球。

5. I'm not really feeling anything loud tonight.
   - 我今晚不太想去太吵的地方。
