# 405-really-into Really Into

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm really into ...
- 意思: 非常喜欢
- 用法: 用于表达强烈的兴趣或热情。

## 例句

1. I'm really into learning new languages.
   - 我真的很喜欢学新语言。

2. I'm really into this band I just discovered.
   - 我真的很喜欢刚发现的这支乐队。

3. I'm really into running in the mornings.
   - 我真的很喜欢早上跑步。

4. I'm really into trying different cuisines.
   - 我真的很喜欢尝试不同的菜系。

5. I'm really into board games with friends.
   - 我真的很喜欢和朋友玩桌游。
