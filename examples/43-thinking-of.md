# 43-thinking-of Thinking Of

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I was thinking we could ...
- 意思: 一起商量计划。
- 用法: 用于提出一个建议。

## 例句

1. I was thinking we could celebrate a little
   - 我在想我们可以小小庆祝一下。

2. I was thinking we could have a quick chat
   - 我在想我们可以聊几句。

3. I was thinking we could stop by later
   - 我在想我们可以待会儿顺路过去。

4. I was thinking we could pick you up
   - 我在想我们可以接你。

5. I was thinking we could swing by the cafe
   - 我在想我们可以顺路去咖啡馆。
