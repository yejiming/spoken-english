# 420-can-t-get-over Can't Get Over

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I just can't get over ...
- 意思: 难以释怀
- 用法: 用于表达对某件事久久不能忘怀。

## 例句

1. I just can't get over how beautiful the sunset was last night.
   - 我就是忘不了昨晚的日落有多美。

2. I just can't get over the fact that he lied to us.
   - 我就是没法释怀他对我们撒了谎。

3. I just can't get over how good that meal was.
   - 我就是忘不了那顿饭有多好吃。

4. I just can't get over the ending of that movie.
   - 我就是没法释怀那部电影的结局。

5. I just can't get over how fast the kids have grown up.
   - 我就是感叹孩子们长得这么快。
