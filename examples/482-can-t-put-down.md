# 482-can-t-put-down Can't Put Down

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can't put down ...
- 意思: 放不下来
- 用法: 用于说某样东西让你爱不释手。

## 例句

1. I can't put down this book — the story is amazing.
   - 这本书我放不下来，故事太精彩了。

2. I can't put down my phone — this game is addictive.
   - 手机放不下来，这游戏太上瘾了。

3. I can't put down this manga series.
   - 这套漫画我放不下来。

4. I can't put down this puzzle — I'm so close to finishing.
   - 这个拼图我放不下来，快完成了。

5. I can't put down the new novel everyone's reading.
   - 大家都在看的新小说我放不下来。
