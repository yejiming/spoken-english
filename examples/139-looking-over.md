# 139-looking-over Looking Over

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I'm looking over ... right now.
- 意思: 在检查审阅
- 用法: 用于说你正在查看或审阅某样东西。

## 例句

1. I'm looking over the contract right now.
   - 我现在在看合同。

2. I'm looking over your resume right now.
   - 我现在在看你的简历。

3. I'm looking over the test results right now.
   - 我现在在看测试结果。

4. I'm looking over the feedback from the client right now.
   - 我现在在看客户的反馈。

5. I'm looking over the meeting agenda right now.
   - 我现在在看会议议程。
