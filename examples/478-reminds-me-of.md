# 478-reminds-me-of Reminds Me Of

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: That reminds me of ...
- 意思: 让我想起
- 用法: 用于说某事让你联想到另一件事。

## 例句

1. That reminds me of the trip we took last summer.
   - 这让我想起去年夏天的旅行。

2. That reminds me of a funny story from high school.
   - 这让我想起高中时一个好笑的事。

3. That reminds me of my grandma's cooking.
   - 这让我想起奶奶做的菜。

4. That reminds me of the old café we used to go to.
   - 这让我想起我们以前常去的那家咖啡店。

5. That reminds me of a song I haven't heard in years.
   - 这让我想起一首好多年没听的歌。
