# 99-leaning-toward Leaning Toward

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm leaning toward ...
- 意思: 偏向某个选择。
- 用法: 用于说你的倾向。

## 例句

1. I'm leaning toward the weather today
   - 我更倾向于今天的天气。

2. I'm leaning toward speaking up in meetings
   - 我更倾向于在会议上发言。

3. I'm leaning toward spending money on that
   - 我更倾向于为那个花钱。

4. I'm leaning toward this kind of music
   - 我更倾向于这种音乐。

5. I'm leaning toward trying something new
   - 我更倾向于尝试新事物。
