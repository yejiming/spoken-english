# 481-gave-it-a-shot Gave It A Shot

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I gave ... a shot and loved it.
- 意思: 试了一下就爱上了
- 用法: 用于说尝试后意外地喜欢。

## 例句

1. I gave rock climbing a shot and loved it.
   - 我试了攀岩就爱上了。

2. I gave journaling a shot and loved it.
   - 我试了写日记就爱上了。

3. I gave cold brew coffee a shot and loved it.
   - 我试了冷萃咖啡就爱上了。

4. I gave pottery a shot and loved it.
   - 我试了陶艺就爱上了。

5. I gave meal prepping a shot and loved it.
   - 我试了提前准备饭菜就爱上了。
