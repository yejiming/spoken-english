# 030-was-just-about-to Was Just About To

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I was just about to ...
- 意思: 我正要去做
- 用法: 用于表示你刚好正要去做某件事。

## 例句

1. I was just about to call you.
   - 我正要给你打电话呢。

2. I was just about to leave the house.
   - 我正要出门呢。

3. I was just about to make some tea.
   - 我正要泡杯茶呢。

4. I was just about to text you back.
   - 我正要回你消息呢。

5. I was just about to take a shower.
   - 我正要去洗澡呢。
