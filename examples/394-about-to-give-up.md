# 394-about-to-give-up About To Give Up

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm about to give up on ...
- 意思: 快要放弃了
- 用法: 用于说对某事快失去耐心要放弃。

## 例句

1. I'm about to give up on fixing this printer.
   - 我快要放弃修这台打印机了。

2. I'm about to give up on learning this software.
   - 我快要放弃学这个软件了。

3. I'm about to give up on finding a parking spot.
   - 我快要放弃找停车位了。

4. I'm about to give up on getting a response from them.
   - 我快要放弃等他们回复了。

5. I'm about to give up on this DIY project.
   - 我快要放弃这个DIY项目了。
