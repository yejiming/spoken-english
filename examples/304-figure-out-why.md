# 304-figure-out-why Figure Out Why

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: Can you help me figure out why ...?
- 意思: 帮我查查原因
- 用法: 用于请别人帮你找出问题的原因。

## 例句

1. Can you help me figure out why the screen keeps flickering?
   - 你能帮我看看屏幕为什么一直闪吗？

2. Can you help me figure out why my emails aren't sending?
   - 你能帮我看看邮件为什么发不出去吗？

3. Can you help me figure out why the internet is so slow?
   - 你能帮我看看网速为什么这么慢吗？

4. Can you help me figure out why the app crashes on startup?
   - 你能帮我看看应用为什么一打开就崩溃吗？

5. Can you help me figure out why the heater isn't turning on?
   - 你能帮我看看暖气为什么打不开吗？
