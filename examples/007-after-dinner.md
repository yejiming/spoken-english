# 007-after-dinner After Dinner

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I like to ... after dinner.
- 意思: 晚饭后的放松动作
- 用法: 用于说晚饭后爱做的事。

## 例句

1. I like to go for a walk after dinner.
   - 我喜欢晚饭后去散步。

2. I like to watch a show after dinner.
   - 我喜欢晚饭后看个节目。

3. I like to have a cup of tea after dinner.
   - 我喜欢晚饭后喝杯茶。

4. I like to do the dishes right after dinner.
   - 我喜欢晚饭后马上洗碗。

5. I like to sit on the balcony after dinner.
   - 我喜欢晚饭后坐在阳台上。
