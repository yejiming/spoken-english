# 22-turn-in Turn In

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I need to turn in ... today.
- 意思: 交作业/交材料。
- 用法: 用于说今天必须提交的东西。

## 例句

1. I need to turn in taking notes today.
   - 我今天得交做笔记。

2. I need to turn in the slides today.
   - 我今天得交这些幻灯片。

3. I need to turn in asking for feedback today.
   - 我今天得交征求反馈。

4. I need to turn in the spreadsheet today.
   - 我今天得交这个表格。

5. I need to turn in the draft today.
   - 我今天得交草稿。
