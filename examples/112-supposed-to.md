# 112-supposed-to Supposed To

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I'm supposed to ... by noon.
- 意思: 中午前得做完
- 用法: 用于说按计划你应该在中午前完成某事。

## 例句

1. I'm supposed to submit the report by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前提交报告。

2. I'm supposed to finish grading the papers by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前把试卷批完。

3. I'm supposed to send out the meeting invite by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前发出会议邀请。

4. I'm supposed to review the pull request by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前审完这个PR。

5. I'm supposed to confirm the reservation by noon.
   - 我应该在中午前确认预订。
