# 431-changed-my-mind Changed My Mind

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I changed my mind about ...
- 意思: 改变想法了
- 用法: 用于说对某事改变了看法。

## 例句

1. I changed my mind about moving to the suburbs.
   - 我改变了搬去郊区的想法。

2. I changed my mind about the blue paint — I want green.
   - 我改变了用蓝漆的想法，我想要绿色。

3. I changed my mind about getting a dog.
   - 我改变了养狗的想法。

4. I changed my mind about skipping the party.
   - 我改变了不去派对的想法。

5. I changed my mind about that restaurant — let's try it.
   - 我改变了对那家餐厅的想法，去试试吧。
