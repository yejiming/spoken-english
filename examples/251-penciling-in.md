# 251-penciling-in Penciling In

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm penciling in ... for Saturday.
- 意思: 暂定安排
- 用法: 用于表示初步把某件事安排在某天。

## 例句

1. I'm penciling in brunch for Saturday.
   - 我暂定周六吃早午餐。

2. I'm penciling in a beach trip for Saturday.
   - 我暂定周六去海边。

3. I'm penciling in a game night for Saturday.
   - 我暂定周六搞游戏之夜。

4. I'm penciling in the farmers market for Saturday.
   - 我暂定周六去农贸市场。

5. I'm penciling in a workout for Saturday.
   - 我暂定周六去锻炼。
