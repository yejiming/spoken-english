# 480-open-minded Open Minded

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm keeping an open mind about ...
- 意思: 保持开放态度
- 用法: 用于说愿意对某事保持开放态度。

## 例句

1. I'm keeping an open mind about the new management.
   - 对新管理层我保持开放态度。

2. I'm keeping an open mind about trying plant-based food.
   - 对尝试素食我保持开放态度。

3. I'm keeping an open mind about the reorganization.
   - 对机构改组我保持开放态度。

4. I'm keeping an open mind about online dating.
   - 对网上交友我保持开放态度。

5. I'm keeping an open mind about the new neighborhood.
   - 对新社区我保持开放态度。
