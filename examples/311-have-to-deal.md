# 311-have-to-deal Have To Deal

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I have to deal with ...
- 意思: 不得不去处理
- 用法: 用于表达你必须处理某个麻烦的事情。

## 例句

1. I have to deal with a broken lock on the front door.
   - 我得处理前门坏掉的锁。

2. I have to deal with a pile of overdue paperwork.
   - 我得处理一堆逾期的文件。

3. I have to deal with the water damage from the leak.
   - 我得处理漏水造成的损坏。

4. I have to deal with a complaint from the landlord.
   - 我得处理房东的投诉。

5. I have to deal with the insurance claim for my car.
   - 我得处理车的保险理赔。
