# 160-over-budget Over Budget

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: We went over budget on ...
- 意思: 超预算了
- 用法: 用于说某个项目或活动花超了预算。

## 例句

1. We went over budget on the conference.
   - 我们在会议上超预算了。

2. We went over budget on the marketing campaign.
   - 我们在营销活动上超预算了。

3. We went over budget on office renovations.
   - 我们在办公室装修上超预算了。

4. We went over budget on the team retreat.
   - 我们在团建上超预算了。

5. We went over budget on hiring contractors.
   - 我们在请外包上超预算了。
