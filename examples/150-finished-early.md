# 150-finished-early Finished Early

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I finished ... earlier than expected.
- 意思: 提前完成了
- 用法: 用于说你比预计更早地完成了某件事。

## 例句

1. I finished the audit earlier than expected.
   - 我比预期更早完成了审计。

2. I finished my homework earlier than expected.
   - 我比预期更早做完了作业。

3. I finished the report earlier than expected.
   - 我比预期更早写完了报告。

4. I finished the migration earlier than expected.
   - 我比预期更早完成了数据迁移。

5. I finished grading the papers earlier than expected.
   - 我比预期更早批完了试卷。
