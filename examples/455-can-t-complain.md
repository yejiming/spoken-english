# 455-can-t-complain Can't Complain

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can't complain about ...
- 意思: 没什么好抱怨的
- 用法: 用于说对某事感到满足。

## 例句

1. I can't complain about the weather this spring.
   - 今年春天的天气没什么好抱怨的。

2. I can't complain about my work-life balance right now.
   - 我现在的工作生活平衡没什么好抱怨的。

3. I can't complain about the food at this hotel.
   - 这个酒店的饭没什么好抱怨的。

4. I can't complain about the commute since I moved.
   - 搬家后通勤没什么好抱怨的。

5. I can't complain about how the project turned out.
   - 项目结果没什么好抱怨的。
