# 340-didn-t-save Didn't Save

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I didn't save ... and lost everything.
- 意思: 没保存全丢了
- 用法: 用于说忘记保存导致内容丢失。

## 例句

1. I didn't save my essay and lost everything.
   - 我没保存论文，全丢了。

2. I didn't save the spreadsheet and lost everything.
   - 我没保存表格，全丢了。

3. I didn't save my project and lost everything.
   - 我没保存项目，全丢了。

4. I didn't save the presentation and lost everything.
   - 我没保存演示文稿，全丢了。

5. I didn't save my design file and lost everything.
   - 我没保存设计文件，全丢了。
