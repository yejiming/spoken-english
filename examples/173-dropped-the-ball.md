# 173-dropped-the-ball Dropped The Ball

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I kinda dropped the ball on ...
- 意思: 搞砸了
- 用法: 用于承认你在某件事上犯了错或没做好。

## 例句

1. I kinda dropped the ball on following up with the client.
   - 我在跟进客户这事上搞砸了。

2. I kinda dropped the ball on submitting the form on time.
   - 我在按时提交表格这事上搞砸了。

3. I kinda dropped the ball on booking the conference room.
   - 我在预定会议室这事上搞砸了。

4. I kinda dropped the ball on updating the spreadsheet.
   - 我在更新表格这事上搞砸了。

5. I kinda dropped the ball on replying to the professor.
   - 我在回复教授这事上搞砸了。
