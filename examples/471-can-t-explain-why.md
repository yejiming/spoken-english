# 471-can-t-explain-why Can't Explain Why

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can't explain why I like ...
- 意思: 说不清为什么
- 用法: 用于说喜欢某事但说不上来原因。

## 例句

1. I can't explain why I like watching people cook online.
   - 我说不清为什么喜欢看别人在线做菜。

2. I can't explain why I like the smell of old books.
   - 我说不清为什么喜欢旧书的味道。

3. I can't explain why I like walking in the rain.
   - 我说不清为什么喜欢在雨中散步。

4. I can't explain why I like airports so much.
   - 我说不清为什么那么喜欢机场。

5. I can't explain why I like reorganizing my room.
   - 我说不清为什么喜欢重新整理房间。
