# 137-missed-deadline Missed Deadline

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I missed the deadline for ...
- 意思: 错过截止日期
- 用法: 用于说你没有按时完成某件事。

## 例句

1. I missed the deadline for the grant application.
   - 我错过了助学金申请的截止日期。

2. I missed the deadline for submitting the report.
   - 我错过了提交报告的截止日期。

3. I missed the deadline for course registration.
   - 我错过了选课的截止日期。

4. I missed the deadline for the proposal by one day.
   - 我晚了一天错过了提案的截止日期。

5. I missed the deadline for turning in my timesheet.
   - 我错过了提交考勤表的截止日期。
