# 463-speaks-to-me Speaks To Me

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: That really speaks to me — ...
- 意思: 特别有共鸣
- 用法: 用于说某事物深深触动了你。

## 例句

1. That really speaks to me — I've felt the same way.
   - 特别有共鸣，我也有过同样的感受。

2. That really speaks to me — the lyrics are so relatable.
   - 特别有共鸣，歌词太贴近生活了。

3. That really speaks to me — I love that philosophy.
   - 特别有共鸣，我很认同这个理念。

4. That really speaks to me — it's exactly how I feel.
   - 特别有共鸣，这正是我的感受。

5. That really speaks to me — the message is powerful.
   - 特别有共鸣，这个信息很有力量。
