# 071-right-before-bed Right Before Bed

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I always ... right before bed.
- 意思: 睡前必做的事
- 用法: 用于描述每天睡前固定会做的事情。

## 例句

1. I always scroll through my phone right before bed.
   - 我睡前总会刷一会儿手机。

2. I always set my alarm right before bed.
   - 我睡前总会定好闹钟。

3. I always drink a glass of water right before bed.
   - 我睡前总会喝杯水。

4. I always read a few pages right before bed.
   - 我睡前总会看几页书。

5. I always do some stretching right before bed.
   - 我睡前总会做做拉伸。
