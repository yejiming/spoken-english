# 244-change-of-plans Change Of Plans

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Change of plans — let's ...
- 意思: 计划有变
- 用法: 用于通知大家计划有了临时变动。

## 例句

1. Change of plans — let's meet at the park instead.
   - 计划有变——咱们改去公园碰面吧。

2. Change of plans — let's do brunch instead of dinner.
   - 计划有变——改吃早午餐吧。

3. Change of plans — let's Uber there.
   - 计划有变——咱们打车去吧。

4. Change of plans — let's go to the new spot.
   - 计划有变——咱们去那个新地方吧。

5. Change of plans — let's just stay in.
   - 计划有变——咱们就待在家吧。
