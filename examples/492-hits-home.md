# 492-hits-home Hits Home

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: That really hits home — ...
- 意思: 说到心坎里了
- 用法: 用于说某事深深触动了你的内心。

## 例句

1. That really hits home — I've been through the same thing.
   - 说到心坎里了，我也经历过同样的事。

2. That really hits home — my family went through that too.
   - 说到心坎里了，我家也经历过。

3. That really hits home — I needed to hear that today.
   - 说到心坎里了，我今天正需要听到这些。

4. That really hits home — it's exactly my situation right now.
   - 说到心坎里了，这正是我现在的处境。

5. That really hits home — I think about that all the time.
   - 说到心坎里了，我一直在想这件事。
