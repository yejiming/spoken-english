# 484-way-better-than Way Better Than

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It's way better to ... than ...
- 意思: 比起来好多了
- 用法: 用于比较两种做法，更推荐前者。

## 例句

1. It's way better to meal prep than to eat out every day.
   - 提前准备比每天外食好多了。

2. It's way better to fix it now than to wait.
   - 现在修比等着好多了。

3. It's way better to ask for help than to struggle alone.
   - 求助比自己硬撑好多了。

4. It's way better to save up than to go into debt.
   - 攒钱比负债好多了。

5. It's way better to walk than to drive in this traffic.
   - 这种路况走路比开车好多了。
