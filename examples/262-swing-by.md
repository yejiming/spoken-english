# 262-swing-by Swing By

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: You should swing by ... later.
- 意思: 过来坐坐
- 用法: 用于随意地邀请别人过来。

## 例句

1. You should swing by my place later.
   - 你待会过来我家坐坐。

2. You should swing by the cookout later.
   - 你待会过来烧烤聚会看看。

3. You should swing by the bar later.
   - 你待会过来酒吧坐坐。

4. You should swing by the office later.
   - 你待会过来办公室坐坐。

5. You should swing by the pool later.
   - 你待会过来游泳池玩玩。
