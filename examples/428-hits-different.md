# 428-hits-different Hits Different

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It hits different when you ...
- 意思: 感觉完全不一样
- 用法: 用于说某体验在特定情况下感觉更好。

## 例句

1. It hits different when you eat ramen on a cold night.
   - 大冷天晚上吃拉面感觉完全不一样。

2. It hits different when you listen to that song alone.
   - 一个人听那首歌感觉完全不一样。

3. It hits different when you finally understand the lyrics.
   - 终于听懂歌词后感觉完全不一样。

4. It hits different when you cook for someone you love.
   - 给喜欢的人做饭感觉完全不一样。

5. It hits different when you come home after a long trip.
   - 长途旅行回到家感觉完全不一样。
