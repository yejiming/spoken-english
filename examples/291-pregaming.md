# 291-pregaming Pregaming

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: We're pregaming at ... first.
- 意思: 先去预热
- 用法: 用于说明正式活动前先在某处聚一下。

## 例句

1. We're pregaming at Kevin's first.
   - 我们先去凯文家预热。

2. We're pregaming at the sports bar first.
   - 我们先去运动酒吧预热。

3. We're pregaming at my apartment first.
   - 我们先在我公寓预热。

4. We're pregaming at the hotel first.
   - 我们先在酒店预热。

5. We're pregaming at the taco spot first.
   - 我们先去墨西哥餐厅预热。
