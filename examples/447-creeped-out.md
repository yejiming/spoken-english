# 447-creeped-out Creeped Out

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm a little creeped out by ...
- 意思: 有点被吓到了
- 用法: 用于说某事让你感到有点毛骨悚然。

## 例句

1. I'm a little creeped out by that abandoned house.
   - 那栋废弃的房子让我有点被吓到了。

2. I'm a little creeped out by how accurate the ad targeting is.
   - 广告推送这么精准让我有点被吓到了。

3. I'm a little creeped out by the noises in the attic.
   - 阁楼的声响让我有点被吓到了。

4. I'm a little creeped out by that realistic robot.
   - 那个逼真的机器人让我有点被吓到了。

5. I'm a little creeped out by how quiet the street is at night.
   - 夜里这条街太安静让我有点被吓到了。
