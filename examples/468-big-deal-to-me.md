# 468-big-deal-to-me Big Deal To Me

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: It's a big deal to me that ...
- 意思: 对我很重要
- 用法: 用于说某件事对你意义重大。

## 例句

1. It's a big deal to me that I got invited.
   - 被邀请对我来说很重要。

2. It's a big deal to me that we hit the goal.
   - 达成目标对我来说很重要。

3. It's a big deal to me that you showed up.
   - 你能来对我来说很重要。

4. It's a big deal to me that I finally graduated.
   - 终于毕业了对我来说很重要。

5. It's a big deal to me that they recognized my work.
   - 他们认可我的工作对我来说很重要。
