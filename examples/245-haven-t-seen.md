# 245-haven-t-seen Haven't Seen

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I haven't seen ... in forever.
- 意思: 好久没见了
- 用法: 用于表示你已经很久没见到某人了。

## 例句

1. I haven't seen Tom in forever.
   - 我好久没见到汤姆了。

2. I haven't seen the whole crew in forever.
   - 我好久没见全部人了。

3. I haven't seen my college friends in forever.
   - 我好久没见大学朋友了。

4. I haven't seen my neighbor in forever.
   - 我好久没见我邻居了。

5. I haven't seen your brother in forever.
   - 我好久没见你哥了。
