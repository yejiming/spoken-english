# 290-bringing-food Bringing Food

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm bringing ... to the party.
- 意思: 我带东西去
- 用法: 用于告知大家你会带什么食物或饮料去聚会。

## 例句

1. I'm bringing chips and guac to the party.
   - 我带薯片和牛油果酱去聚会。

2. I'm bringing a bottle of wine to the party.
   - 我带瓶红酒去聚会。

3. I'm bringing brownies to the party.
   - 我带布朗尼去聚会。

4. I'm bringing a fruit platter to the party.
   - 我带水果拼盘去聚会。

5. I'm bringing some craft beer to the party.
   - 我带点精酿啤酒去聚会。
