# 495-didn-t-expect Didn't Expect

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I didn't expect to like ...
- 意思: 没想到会喜欢
- 用法: 用于说意外地喜欢上了某事物。

## 例句

1. I didn't expect to like hiking this much.
   - 没想到我会这么喜欢爬山。

2. I didn't expect to like the new flavor of chips.
   - 没想到我会喜欢新口味的薯片。

3. I didn't expect to like working night shifts.
   - 没想到我会喜欢上夜班。

4. I didn't expect to like that documentary.
   - 没想到我会喜欢那部纪录片。

5. I didn't expect to like living in a smaller city.
   - 没想到我会喜欢住在小城市。
