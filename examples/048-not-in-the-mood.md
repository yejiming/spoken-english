# 048-not-in-the-mood Not In The Mood

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm not in the mood to ...
- 意思: 没心情做某事
- 用法: 用于表达当前没有做某件事的心情。

## 例句

1. I'm not in the mood to talk right now.
   - 我现在没心情说话。

2. I'm not in the mood to go anywhere.
   - 我没心情去任何地方。

3. I'm not in the mood to deal with this.
   - 我没心情处理这件事。

4. I'm not in the mood to socialize.
   - 我没心情社交。

5. I'm not in the mood to think about it.
   - 我没心情想这件事。
