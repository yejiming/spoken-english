# 58-keep-open Keep Open

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'm just trying to keep ... open.
- 意思: 保留时间。
- 用法: 用于先不要排满。

## 例句

1. I'm just trying to keep make a reservation open.
   - 我只是想把订座位先留空。

2. I'm just trying to keep bring something to share open.
   - 我只是想把带点吃的分享先留空。

3. I'm just trying to keep take a rain check open.
   - 我只是想把改天再约先留空。

4. I'm just trying to keep stay in tonight open.
   - 我只是想把今晚待在家先留空。

5. I'm just trying to keep head to the airport open.
   - 我只是想把去机场先留空。
