# 490-gut-feeling Gut Feeling

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I have a gut feeling about ...
- 意思: 有种直觉
- 用法: 用于说凭直觉感知到某事。

## 例句

1. I have a gut feeling about this project going well.
   - 我有种直觉这个项目会顺利。

2. I have a gut feeling about that restaurant being good.
   - 我有种直觉那家餐厅不错。

3. I have a gut feeling about the interview going badly.
   - 我有种直觉面试不太好。

4. I have a gut feeling about this deal being too good.
   - 我有种直觉这笔交易好得不太真实。

5. I have a gut feeling about something being off.
   - 我有种直觉有什么不对劲。
