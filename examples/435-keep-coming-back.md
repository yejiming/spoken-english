# 435-keep-coming-back Keep Coming Back

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I keep coming back to ...
- 意思: 总是会回来
- 用法: 用于说反复被某事物吸引。

## 例句

1. I keep coming back to this playlist when I study.
   - 学习时我总是会回来听这个歌单。

2. I keep coming back to this café for the vibe.
   - 因为氛围我总是会回来这家咖啡店。

3. I keep coming back to the idea of starting a blog.
   - 开博客这个想法我总是会想起来。

4. I keep coming back to this show — it never gets old.
   - 这部剧我总是会重新看，百看不厌。

5. I keep coming back to running as my go-to workout.
   - 跑步始终是我最常回来做的运动。
