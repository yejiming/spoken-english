# 020-winding-down Winding Down

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I'm winding down by ...
- 意思: 晚上收尾放松
- 用法: 用于说你晚上怎么放松下来。

## 例句

1. I'm winding down by reading a few chapters.
   - 我正在读几章书放松一下。

2. I'm winding down by listening to some jazz.
   - 我正在听爵士乐放松一下。

3. I'm winding down by taking a warm bath.
   - 我正在泡个热水澡放松一下。

4. I'm winding down by doing a little stretching.
   - 我正在做做拉伸放松一下。

5. I'm winding down by watching a cooking show.
   - 我正在看美食节目放松一下。
