# 066-talked-myself-into Talked Myself Into

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I talked myself into ...
- 意思: 说服自己去做
- 用法: 用于描述你最终说服了自己去做某件原本犹豫的事。

## 例句

1. I talked myself into going for a walk.
   - 我说服自己去散了个步。

2. I talked myself into trying that new cafe.
   - 我说服自己去试了那家新咖啡店。

3. I talked myself into waking up earlier.
   - 我说服自己早起了。

4. I talked myself into signing up for a class.
   - 我说服自己报了个班。

5. I talked myself into cooking instead of ordering.
   - 我说服自己做饭而不是叫外卖。
