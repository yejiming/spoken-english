# 396-won-t-connect Won't Connect

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: My ... won't connect to ...
- 意思: 连接不上
- 用法: 用于说设备无法连接到另一设备。

## 例句

1. My phone won't connect to the car's Bluetooth.
   - 我的手机连接不上车载蓝牙。

2. My laptop won't connect to the projector.
   - 我的笔记本连接不上投影仪。

3. My speaker won't connect to the Wi-Fi.
   - 我的音箱连接不上Wi-Fi。

4. My watch won't connect to my phone anymore.
   - 我的手表连接不上手机了。

5. My printer won't connect to the new network.
   - 我的打印机连接不上新网络。
