# 52-rather-than-stay-in Rather Than Stay In

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: I'd rather ... than stay home.
- 意思: 比较两个选择。
- 用法: 用于表达更想出去。

## 例句

1. I'd rather take a rain check than stay home.
   - 我宁愿改天再约，也不想待在家。

2. I'd rather stay in tonight than stay home.
   - 我宁愿今晚待在家，也不想待在家。

3. I'd rather head to the airport than stay home.
   - 我宁愿去机场，也不想待在家。

4. I'd rather join us after work than stay home.
   - 我宁愿下班后加入我们，也不想待在家。

5. I'd rather split the bill than stay home.
   - 我宁愿平摊账单，也不想待在家。
