# 97-not-convinced Not Convinced

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm not convinced that ...
- 意思: 还没被说服。
- 用法: 用于表达怀疑。

## 例句

1. I'm not convinced that keeping things simple
   - 我还不太相信保持简单。

2. I'm not convinced that a crowded room
   - 我还不太相信一个拥挤的房间。

3. I'm not convinced that the new layout
   - 我还不太相信新版布局。

4. I'm not convinced that getting up early
   - 我还不太相信早起。

5. I'm not convinced that sharing honest feedback
   - 我还不太相信给出坦诚反馈。
