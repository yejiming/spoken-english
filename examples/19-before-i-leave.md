# 19-before-i-leave Before I Leave

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I have to ... before I leave.
- 意思: 出门前必须做。
- 用法: 用于说离开前的必要动作。

## 例句

1. I have to turn on some music before I leave.
   - 我离开前得先放点音乐。

2. I have to read a few pages before I leave.
   - 我离开前得先读几页书。

3. I have to make the bed before I leave.
   - 我离开前得先整理床铺。

4. I have to head out early before I leave.
   - 我离开前得先早点出门。

5. I have to heat up leftovers before I leave.
   - 我离开前得先热一下剩菜。
