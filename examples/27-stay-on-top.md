# 27-stay-on-top Stay On Top

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I'm trying to stay on top of ...
- 意思: 不想漏掉进度。
- 用法: 用于说你要保持掌控。

## 例句

1. I'm trying to stay on top of the assignment
   - 我想把作业都跟上。

2. I'm trying to stay on top of the presentation
   - 我想把演示稿都跟上。

3. I'm trying to stay on top of finishing this report
   - 我想把完成这份报告都跟上。

4. I'm trying to stay on top of replying to those emails
   - 我想把回复那些邮件都跟上。

5. I'm trying to stay on top of the meeting prep
   - 我想把会议准备都跟上。
