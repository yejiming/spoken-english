# 438-caught-off-guard Caught Off Guard

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I was caught off guard by ...
- 意思: 被打了个措手不及
- 用法: 用于说被意想不到的事惊到了。

## 例句

1. I was caught off guard by the surprise party.
   - 突然的惊喜派对让我措手不及。

2. I was caught off guard by the question during the interview.
   - 面试中那个问题让我措手不及。

3. I was caught off guard by the price increase.
   - 涨价让我措手不及。

4. I was caught off guard by how emotional the speech was.
   - 那段演讲的感人程度让我措手不及。

5. I was caught off guard by the sudden schedule change.
   - 突然的日程变动让我措手不及。
