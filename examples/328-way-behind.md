# 328-way-behind Way Behind

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: I'm way behind on fixing ...
- 意思: 修理进度落后
- 用法: 用于说修理或解决问题的进度落后。

## 例句

1. I'm way behind on fixing the kitchen sink.
   - 我修厨房水槽的进度严重落后。

2. I'm way behind on fixing all the bugs in the code.
   - 我修代码bug的进度太落后了。

3. I'm way behind on fixing the fence in the backyard.
   - 我修后院栅栏的进度落后了。

4. I'm way behind on fixing the leaky roof.
   - 我修漏水屋顶的进度拖了很久。

5. I'm way behind on fixing my bike before the trip.
   - 出发前我还没来得及修好自行车。
