# 298-keep-me-posted Keep Me Posted

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Keep me posted on ...
- 意思: 有消息告诉我
- 用法: 用于让对方有最新消息时通知你。

## 例句

1. Keep me posted on the time.
   - 时间定了告诉我。

2. Keep me posted on the restaurant.
   - 餐厅定了告诉我。

3. Keep me posted on the guest list.
   - 参加的人定了告诉我。

4. Keep me posted on the details.
   - 有细节了告诉我。

5. Keep me posted on the location.
   - 地点定了告诉我。
