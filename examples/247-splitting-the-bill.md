# 247-splitting-the-bill Splitting The Bill

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Are we splitting ... or what?
- 意思: 怎么买单
- 用法: 用于确认大家怎么分摊费用。

## 例句

1. Are we splitting the bill or what?
   - 咱们分摊还是怎么着？

2. Are we splitting the Uber or what?
   - 打车费分摊不？

3. Are we splitting the hotel room or what?
   - 酒店房费分摊不？

4. Are we splitting groceries or what?
   - 食材费用分摊不？

5. Are we splitting the tickets or what?
   - 票钱分摊不？
