# 325-broke-down Broke Down

- 场景: Problems & Solutions / 问题处理
- 句型: My ... just broke down.
- 意思: 刚刚坏了
- 用法: 用于说设备突然坏掉。

## 例句

1. My washing machine just broke down mid-cycle.
   - 我的洗衣机洗到一半就坏了。

2. My car just broke down on the highway.
   - 我的车刚在高速上坏了。

3. My AC just broke down in the middle of summer.
   - 我的空调大夏天突然坏了。

4. My dishwasher just broke down after one year.
   - 我的洗碗机才一年就坏了。

5. My treadmill just broke down during my workout.
   - 我的跑步机锻炼到一半坏了。
