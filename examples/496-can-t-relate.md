# 496-can-t-relate Can't Relate

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I can't really relate to ...
- 意思: 没有共鸣
- 用法: 用于说无法理解或感同身受。

## 例句

1. I can't really relate to people who hate mornings.
   - 对讨厌早上的人我没有共鸣。

2. I can't really relate to the hype about that show.
   - 对那部剧的热度我没有共鸣。

3. I can't really relate to wanting to be famous.
   - 对想出名这件事我没有共鸣。

4. I can't really relate to the whole influencer culture.
   - 对网红文化我没有共鸣。

5. I can't really relate to working from home all the time.
   - 对一直在家办公我没有共鸣。
