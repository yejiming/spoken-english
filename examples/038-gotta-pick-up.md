# 038-gotta-pick-up Gotta Pick Up

- 场景: Daily Life / 日常生活
- 句型: I gotta pick up ... on the way.
- 意思: 顺路买一下
- 用法: 用于表示你打算在路上顺便带某样东西。

## 例句

1. I gotta pick up some milk on the way.
   - 我得顺路买点牛奶。

2. I gotta pick up my prescription on the way.
   - 我得顺路拿一下处方药。

3. I gotta pick up some snacks on the way.
   - 我得顺路买点零食。

4. I gotta pick up dinner on the way.
   - 我得顺路买点晚饭。

5. I gotta pick up dog food on the way.
   - 我得顺路买点狗粮。
