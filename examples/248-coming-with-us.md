# 248-coming-with-us Coming With Us

- 场景: Social Plans / 社交安排
- 句型: Are you coming with us to ...?
- 意思: 跟我们去吗
- 用法: 用于邀请或确认对方是否一起去某个地方。

## 例句

1. Are you coming with us to the game?
   - 你跟我们去看比赛吗？

2. Are you coming with us to karaoke?
   - 你跟我们去唱歌吗？

3. Are you coming with us to the beach?
   - 你跟我们去海边吗？

4. Are you coming with us to the escape room?
   - 你跟我们去密室逃脱吗？

5. Are you coming with us to the food festival?
   - 你跟我们去美食节吗？
