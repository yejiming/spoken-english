# 409-worth-it Worth It

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I think it's worth ...
- 意思: 觉得值得
- 用法: 用于评价某事是否值得做。

## 例句

1. I think it's worth trying that new restaurant downtown.
   - 我觉得值得去试试市中心那家新餐厅。

2. I think it's worth investing time in learning a new skill.
   - 我觉得花时间学一门新技能是值得的。

3. I think it's worth having an honest conversation with her.
   - 我觉得值得跟她坦诚聊一聊。

4. I think it's worth spending a bit more for better quality.
   - 我觉得多花一点钱买好品质是值得的。

5. I think it's worth giving him a second chance.
   - 我觉得值得给他再一次机会。
