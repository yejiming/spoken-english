# 442-started-enjoying Started Enjoying

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I've started to enjoy ...
- 意思: 开始享受了
- 用法: 用于说开始喜欢上以前不喜欢的事。

## 例句

1. I've started to enjoy waking up early.
   - 我开始享受早起了。

2. I've started to enjoy working out after work.
   - 我开始享受下班后健身了。

3. I've started to enjoy meal prepping on Sundays.
   - 我开始享受周日做一周的饭了。

4. I've started to enjoy listening to podcasts.
   - 我开始享受听播客了。

5. I've started to enjoy my morning walks.
   - 我开始享受早上散步了。
