# 432-on-the-fence On The Fence

- 场景: Feelings & Opinions / 感受观点
- 句型: I'm on the fence about ...
- 意思: 还在犹豫
- 用法: 用于说对某事拿不定主意。

## 例句

1. I'm on the fence about taking that job offer.
   - 我还在犹豫要不要接那份工作。

2. I'm on the fence about cutting my hair short.
   - 我还在犹豫要不要剪短发。

3. I'm on the fence about buying the new phone.
   - 我还在犹豫要不要买新手机。

4. I'm on the fence about signing up for the marathon.
   - 我还在犹豫要不要报名马拉松。

5. I'm on the fence about switching to a different gym.
   - 我还在犹豫要不要换健身房。
