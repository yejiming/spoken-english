# 161-delegated Delegated

- 场景: Work & School / 工作学习
- 句型: I delegated ... to someone else.
- 意思: 委派给别人了
- 用法: 用于说你把某项工作交给了别人去做。

## 例句

1. I delegated the data cleanup to someone else.
   - 我把数据清理委派给别人了。

2. I delegated the scheduling to someone else.
   - 我把排日程的事委派给别人了。

3. I delegated the follow-up calls to someone else.
   - 我把跟进电话委派给别人了。

4. I delegated the onboarding tasks to someone else.
   - 我把入职培训任务委派给别人了。

5. I delegated the report formatting to someone else.
   - 我把报告排版委派给别人了。
